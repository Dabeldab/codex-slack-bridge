Q: Where is the Inventory Change Log in New Nova?

A: In the blue menu, the screen appears as Change Log.

Log in to New Nova.
Select the correct Region.
Click the blue menu button.
Click Change Log.

Use this screen when the customer asks who changed inventory, when a quantity changed, or why stock moved.

Q: What is displayed on the Change Log?

A: Source templates show the Inventory Change Log can include:

Product.
Date.
Location.
Performed by.
Comments.
Cost.
Quantity.
Log Type.

These fields help explain inventory history. For example, a product quantity may have changed because of a count, loss report, transfer, purchase order receiving, or manual update.

Q: How do I find who changed inventory?

A:

Open Change Log from the blue menu.
Filter or search for the product if the screen provides search/filter controls.
Review Date and Location.
Check Performed by.
Read Comments and Log Type.
Compare the Quantity and Cost fields if the customer is asking about the size or value of the change.

If the customer asks why the inventory report changed, check the Change Log, Transfers, Purchase Orders, Inventory Counts, and Report Loss activity for the same date range.

Q: Why does inventory history not match what the customer expected?

A: Start by checking scope.

Confirm the customer is looking at the same Region and Location.
Confirm the product name, variant, barcode, and category.
Review Change Log for the product.
Check whether a transfer or purchase order was received after the customer last checked inventory.
Check whether a loss/tester/damage report reduced the quantity.
Check whether an inventory count adjusted the quantity.

If the customer is comparing Classic and New Nova, remind them that New Nova may expose the current inventory workflow and report layout differently, but the inventory history should still be explained by the same business events.
