Q: Where are invoices in New Nova?

A: Invoices can be reached from several New Nova screens.

Common paths:

Dashboard invoice tables.
Customers > open customer dashboard > Invoices.
Direct invoice links opened from inside New Nova.

Do not paste direct invoice routes into the browser. Open invoices from the clicked New Nova screen because direct route refreshes can show a 404.

Q: What is displayed when viewing an invoice?

A: Source templates show invoice view/edit areas for:

Summary.
Products.
Payments.
Users or employees.
Refunds.
Coupons.
Totals.
Reviewed?.
Invoice notes/comments when available.
Product commission details.
Payment details.
Refund details.

Use the invoice view when the customer needs to review what happened. Use invoice edit only when the user has permission and the business really needs a correction.

Q: How do I email or print an invoice or receipt in New Nova?

A:

Open the invoice from Dashboard or from the customer's dashboard.
Confirm the invoice number and customer.
Use the available print/email/PDF action on the invoice screen.
If emailing, confirm the customer email is correct.
If printing, confirm the browser/printer dialog opens and the correct printer is selected.
If the action fails, refresh through the blue menu and reopen the invoice.

If Your Session Has Expired appears, log in again before retrying print/email.

Q: Why is the Refund button unavailable or why can't I complete a refund?

A: Check invoice status, payment method, and user permissions.

Open the invoice.
Review Payments and Refunds.
Confirm the invoice has a payment that can be refunded.
Confirm the user has refund permission in Users > System Access.
Confirm the payment method supports the refund path.
For card payments, confirm whether the refund must go through the original processor/terminal flow.
If the invoice is already refunded, reviewed, deleted, or locked, do not force another refund without support review.

If the user sees the invoice but cannot refund, it is often permission-related.

Q: What should I do if an invoice was duplicated or made by mistake?

A: Do not delete or change invoices silently.

Open both invoices if there are duplicates.
Compare customer, date/time, products, payments, tips, taxes, and totals.
Decide whether the correction should be a refund, void/edit path, or support escalation.
Add invoice notes explaining the correction when the screen allows notes.
Review reporting impact before changing a prior-day invoice.
If the user does not have permission, update Users > System Access or have a manager handle it.

If the invoice has already affected EOD, payroll, commission, or payment settlement, be cautious and document the correction.

Q: How do I add or review invoice notes?

A:

Open the invoice from inside New Nova.
Review the invoice summary/edit area.
Use the notes/comments area when available.
Enter a clear customer-facing correction note.
Save only if the change is intentional.

Good notes explain what changed and why. Avoid vague notes such as "fixed" when correcting invoice history.

Q: How do I let a user refund, edit commissions, or edit invoices?

A: Update the user's access.

Open Users from the blue menu.
Open the employee.
Go to System Access.
Enable the needed access, such as refund permission, invoice edit permission, or commission edit permission.
Save.
Have the user log out and log back in.

Give the user only the permissions they need. Invoice/refund/commission permissions affect money and reporting.
