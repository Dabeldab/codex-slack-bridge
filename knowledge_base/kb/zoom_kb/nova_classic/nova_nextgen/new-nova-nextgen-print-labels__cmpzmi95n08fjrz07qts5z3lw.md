Q: How do I open Print Labels?

A:

Log in to New Nova.
Select a Region.
Click the blue menu button in the top-right.
Click Print Labels.

Open it from the clicked blue menu. Do not paste the label route directly into the browser.

Q: How do I print product labels?

A:

Open Print Labels.
Select the location.
Choose Simple or Detailed.
Choose the paper size.
Check or uncheck Print Product Price.
Check or uncheck Print Product Desc.
Filter the product list if needed.
Enter Quantity to Print for each product, or use Auto Fill.
Click Print Labels.

If New Nova opens the Print Product Labels modal, review Text to Print, Quantity, Select Paper Size, Print Product Price, and Print Product Desc., then click Print.

Q: What does Auto Fill do on Print Labels?

A: Auto Fill helps fill label quantities from the listed product quantities.

Open Print Labels.
Select the location.
Filter to the products that need labels.
Click Auto Fill.
Review Quantity to Print before printing.

Do not print until the quantities are correct.

Q: What warnings can appear when printing product labels?

A: The product label modal can warn about barcode length or barcode format.

Warnings can include:

Barcode is too long to print on [paper size] paper.
Barcode will not print because it is not [required format].

If the warning appears:

Choose a different paper size, or
Shorten/correct the barcode, or
Print without the barcode if the business accepts that label.

The barcode warning can also show the maximum allowed character count for the selected paper size.

Q: Can I print labels from Inventory instead?

A: Yes. On the Inventory screen, selected products can use the batch action Print Labels, and individual products can use the row action Print Label.

Open Inventory.
Select a location.
Select product rows.
Choose Print Labels from the batch menu, or open a row Action menu and choose Print Label.
Q: Why can't I print labels from New Nova?

A: Check the label workflow before blaming the printer.

Open Print Labels from the blue menu, or open Inventory and use Print Label / Print Labels from a product or selected products.
Select the correct Region and Location.
Select the products.
Enter Quantity to Print.
Choose Select Paper Size.
Confirm whether Print Product Price and Print Product Desc. should be checked.
Click Print Labels or Print.
Allow browser pop-ups/print dialogs if the browser blocks them.
Confirm the printer driver and paper size match the label stock.

If Your Session Has Expired appears, log in again and reopen the screen from the blue menu.

Q: Why are labels blank, cut off, or missing barcodes?

A: Check the label setup and product data.

Confirm the selected paper size matches the actual labels.
Check browser print scaling and printer page settings.
Confirm the product has a valid barcode.
Review barcode warnings, such as barcode too long or not in the required format.
Confirm whether Print Product Price and Print Product Desc. should be enabled.
Try printing one product first before printing a batch.

If only one product has a problem, check that product's barcode, name, description, and price. If every label has a problem, check printer/page setup.

Q: Which label printer should I use with New Nova?

A: New Nova prepares the label data and opens the print workflow. Printer compatibility depends on the supported printer model, installed driver, browser print settings, and label paper size.

For support, ask for:

Printer model.
Label paper size.
Whether the label is blank, cut off, or not printing.
Whether the problem happens for one product or all products.
Whether the browser print dialog appears.
Review paper size, price, description, and quantity before printing.
