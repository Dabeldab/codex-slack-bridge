Q: How do I open Customers?

A:

Log in to New Nova.
Select a Region.
Click the blue menu button in the top-right.
Click Customers.

Open Customers from the clicked blue menu. Direct customer routes should not be pasted into the browser.

Q: How do I add a customer?

A:

Open Customers.
Click the page-action dropdown in the upper-right of the Customers header.
Click Add Customer.
Fill in Customer Information.
Enter name, phone, email, and birthday details when needed.
Select Gender if needed:
M
F
Add Custom Fields if needed.
Fill in Shipping Information if needed.
Select Loyalty Program if applicable.
Save the customer.

The customer modal can show Customer Information, Shipping Information, Loyalty Program, Custom Fields, Month, Day, Year, Cancel, and save/select controls.

Q: How do I choose which customer fields are required?

A:

Open Customers.
Click the page-action dropdown in the upper-right of the Customers header.
Click Settings.
Review the customer mandatory field screen.
Select the fields employees must complete.
Save.

Use this when employees are allowed to add incomplete customer records and the business wants required fields.

The settings modal can show Select the mandatory fields that will be required when adding a customer, with sections for Customer information, Shipping information, Loyalty Program, and Company Settings.

Q: How do I export customers?

A: New Nova supports exporting selected customers.

Open Customers.
Use Add a Filter if needed.
Select the customers to export.
Use the batch action Export Customers.
Download/open the generated Excel file.

The source controller confirms the export batch action downloads a generated file after the export succeeds.

Q: How do I open a customer dashboard?

A:

Open Customers.
Search or filter for the customer.
Open the row Action menu.
Click View Customer or Open Customer Dashboard.

The customer dashboard can show customer details, invoices, products, layaways, files, and loyalty information depending on the customer record.

Customer dashboard invoice rows can show Invoice#, Location, Sold by, Time and Date, and Total.

Save.

Q: How do I bulk change loyalty programs for customers?

A: Use this only after selecting the correct customers.

Open Customers.
Filter the list if needed.
Select the customers to update.
Choose the bulk loyalty change action if available.
In Select Loyalty Program, choose the new program.
Click Change.
Confirm the customer list afterward.

Common mistake: changing loyalty in bulk without filtering first can update more customers than intended.

Q: How do I open Loyalty Programs?

A:

Click Loyalty Programs.

Q: How do I create a loyalty program?

A:

Open Loyalty Programs.
Click the page-action dropdown in the upper-right of the Loyalty Programs header.
Click New Loyalty Program.
Enter Loyalty Program Name.
Use Select Image if the program needs an image.
Configure Automatic Enrollment.
Enter Minimum Amount Spent for Membership if the customer must spend a minimum amount to join.
Configure Loyalty Points.
Set Earning Points.
Set Using Points.
Configure Benefits.
Add benefits with Add Benefit if needed.
Select a Coupon Name and Quantity for benefits if needed.
Save.

The New Loyalty Program screen can show Summary, Loyalty Points, Benefits, Select Image, Loyalty Program Name, Automatic Enrollment, Minimum Amount Spent for Membership, Save, and Back.

Q: What does Automatic Enrollment do on a loyalty program?

A: Automatic Enrollment controls whether customers are automatically added to the loyalty program when they meet the program requirements.

Open Loyalty Programs.
Add or edit the loyalty program.
In Summary, review Automatic Enrollment.
Turn it on only if the business wants eligible customers added automatically.
Review Minimum Amount Spent for Membership.
Save.

Common mistake: leaving automatic enrollment on for a program that should require employee approval or a loyalty card process.

Q: How do loyalty benefits connect to coupons?

A: Loyalty benefits can use coupons as rewards.

Open Loyalty Programs.
Add or edit the program.
Go to Benefits.
Add a benefit.
Choose the Coupon Name that should be given as the benefit.
Set the benefit Quantity.
Save.

Create and test the coupon first under Coupons before using it as a loyalty benefit.

Q: What does Enter Loyalty Card # mean?

A: New Nova can ask for a loyalty card number.

Enter the card number in Enter Loyalty Card #.
Click OK.
If the customer does not have a card, click No Card if that option is appropriate.

Use the business policy for whether customers can continue without a physical loyalty card.

Q: How do I export a customer or CRM list to Excel?

A:

Open Customers from the blue menu.
Filter or search the customer list.
Select the customers to include, or confirm that the export should include the visible/listed customers.
Use the customer export action.
Open the downloaded Excel file.

If the customer only wants a single location, employee, or campaign segment, apply filters first when available. If the needed filter is not available on Customers, use a report workflow instead of forcing the customer export.

Q: Why does my customer export look wrong or too large?

A: Check selection and filters.

Confirm the correct Region.
Confirm whether the user selected specific rows or exported the full list.
Confirm the customer filters before exporting.
Check whether the customer expected a CRM report instead of the raw customer list.
Open the Excel file and check whether columns were interpreted or reformatted by Excel.

If the customer expected a report by location, employee, or date, use the correct report/export workflow rather than the basic customer export.

Q: Can I export customers for one location or employee?

A: Use available filters first.

Open Customers.
Filter the list by the available location, employee, or customer fields.
Select the matching customers.
Export.

If the screen does not provide the exact filter the customer needs, route the request to reporting. Do not promise that the customer export can filter by every business field.

Q: How do I add custom customer fields?

A: Source templates show a custom-field workflow.

Open Customers.
Open Settings from the page action menu.
Use the custom field option when available.
Click New Custom Field.
Enter Field Name.
Choose Field Type.
Save.

Use mandatory-field settings when the customer wants to require existing customer fields. Use custom fields when the customer needs a new field that does not already exist.
