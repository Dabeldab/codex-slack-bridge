Nova Classic Desktop Download — Overview

This article covers how to download, install, and add your account to Nova Classic — the established version of Nova POS for Windows. After installation you will need your Company Username and Company Password (also called the Access Key) to link the application to your Nova account. Both values are in the welcome email you received when your account was created.

Where to Download Nova Classic

There are three ways to get the Nova Classic installer. Any of them will download the same setup.exe file. Use whichever is most convenient.

Method 1: Direct download link

Open a browser on the computer you want to install Nova on and go to:

https://pos.novapointofsale.com/novapos/Alpha-2025/setup.exe

The browser will prompt you to save the file. Save it and proceed to installation.

Method 2: From your welcome email

When your Nova account was first created, a welcome email was sent to your registration address (subject: “Welcome to Nova Point of Sale!” from donotreply@novapointofsale.com).

Open the welcome email.
Scroll to the buttons section below the Trial Expiry Date box.
Click Download NOVA’s Storefront (the red outlined button on the right side).
The installer downloads to your Downloads folder.

Your Company Username and Company Password are also in this same email — you will need them after installation.

Method 3: From the Nova website
Open a browser and go to https://novapointofsale.com/ .
Scroll to the very bottom of the homepage.
Find the Download Nova POS link in the footer.
Click it to start the download.
Installing Nova Classic
Step 1: Run the installer
Open the downloaded setup.exe file from your Downloads folder.
Follow the Windows installation prompts.
When installation finishes, a Nova shortcut appears on your desktop.
Step 2: Launch Nova for the first time

Double-click the Nova shortcut on your desktop. The Storefront login screen opens.

What the login screen looks like:

Location	What you see
Top-left corner	Access Back Office button (small, person icon)
Top-right corner	NOVA Retail Technology Solutions logo
Left side	Illustration of cashier and customer + “Welcome To Nova storefront” label
Right side, top	A round pill button labeled ADD NEW ACCOUNT with a + icon
Right side, below	Company Name label, Region dropdown, Location dropdown, Password field
Bottom-right	Fingerprint icon (small round button) and Login button (large red)

On a fresh install the Region, Location, and Password fields are empty. You cannot sign in until you add your account first.

Step 3: Add your account (one-time setup)

Click ADD NEW ACCOUNT (the round + pill button at the top of the right-side panel).

The Add Your Account screen appears with two fields: | Field | What to enter | |-------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------| | Company Name* | Your Company Username from the welcome email | | Access Key* | Your Company Password from the welcome email — include any = symbols exactly as shown |

Click the eye icon on the Access Key field to verify what you typed matches the email.

Click the red Save button.

A green “Account added successfully” toast appears in the top-right corner.

You return to the login screen with your company name now shown as a pill button.

This step is done once per computer . You will not enter these credentials again on this device.

Step 4: Sign in

After your account is added:

Select your Region from the Region dropdown.
Select your Location from the Location dropdown.
Optionally check “Use only with [Location]” if this device should always sign in to the same location.
Click the large red Login button.
The Sign In dialog appears (“Nice to see you again”):
Enter your personal Password (your own password, not the Company Password from the email).
Click Sign In . Or use Login with fingerprint on the right side if biometric login is set up.
Company Credentials Required for Installation

The Company Username and Company Password (Access Key) from your welcome email are the only credentials that can add your Nova account to a new device. You cannot complete installation without them.

What each value is
Value	Also called	What it does
Company Username	Company Name	Identifies your Nova business account
Company Password	Access Key, Company Key	Authenticates the account on this device
Critical rules
Copy the = symbols exactly. The Company Password (Access Key) may include = characters at the start, end, or both. These are part of the key and must not be removed.
These are install-time credentials only. After adding your account, daily sign-in uses your personal User Name and Password (created in Back Office → Users → Settings) — not the Company credentials.
Treat these as sensitive. Anyone with both values can install Nova as your business on a new device.
Common installation mistakes
Mistake	Result	Fix
Copied Access Key without = symbols	“Invalid credentials” error	Re-copy the full value from the email including =
Pasted value with leading or trailing spaces	“Invalid credentials” error	Delete whitespace — use the eye icon to verify
Entered personal password in the Access Key field	“Invalid credentials” error	Use the Company Password from the email, not your personal password
Entered Company Username in Access Key field	“Invalid credentials” error	Company Username → Company Name field; Company Password → Access Key field
Finding Your Company Username and Password
Check the welcome email
Search your inbox for: sender donotreply@novapointofsale.com, subject “Welcome to Nova Point of Sale!”.
Also check your spam or junk folder.
Check the email address used when the Nova account was originally created — this may differ from the email you use today.
Open the email and find the green credentials box labeled “Your account is active and now ready for installation.” Your Company Username and Company Password are listed there.
If the email cannot be found

Contact Nova support at 1-800-370-6682, Option 3 (available seven days a week). See the next section for what support will need.

If You Don’t Have Your Credentials

If you cannot locate the welcome email or the Company Username and Password, the Nova support team can help retrieve or resend your credentials.

What support will do

Support will verify your identity and account information before providing credentials. They may also need to obtain approval from the person in charge of the account .

What to have ready
Business name associated with the Nova account
Email address used to sign up
Name of the account owner or authorized contact
How to reach support

Call 1-800-370-6682 and select Option 3 . Available seven days a week .

Troubleshooting: Nova Classic Installation
The Access Key is not being accepted
Re-copy the Access Key from the welcome email — include = symbols at start and end.
Click the eye icon in the Access Key field to read what was entered and compare it to the email exactly.
Check for extra spaces before or after the value.
Confirm the Company Username is in the Company Name field and the Company Password is in the Access Key field — not swapped.
Confirm you are using the Company Password from the email, not your personal Nova user password.
If still failing, contact support at 1-800-370-6682 Option 3.
The installer won’t run
Right-click the setup.exe and choose Run as administrator .
Temporarily disable antivirus software if it is blocking the installer; re-enable after install completes.
Confirm the download finished completely — check the file size in Downloads.
Confirm the computer is running Windows — Nova Classic is Windows-only.
“Account added successfully” appeared but I still can’t sign in

Adding the account links the device to your company. Daily sign-in is a separate step. After the toast appears, select your Region and Location from the dropdowns and click Login. The Sign In dialog will open where you choose your personal User Name and enter your personal Password.

No users appear in the User Name dropdown

No users have been created yet for this Location. Sign in to the Back Office (browser or the Access Back Office button top-left of the Storefront screen), go to Users → Settings, and create at least one user.

I want this computer to always sign in to the same location

On the Storefront login screen (after adding your account), check the “Use only with [Location]” checkbox before clicking Login. This locks the device to that Location going forward.
