Q: How do I make Nova print two receipts for every sale?

A: Go to the payment screen while checking out a sale. Look under the Print and Email button. Turn on the Print 2 copies checkbox. Complete a test sale or reprint to confirm two receipts print. If only specific sale types print twice, note the sale type and contact support because some workflows may have fixed behavior.

Q: Can I enter older sales into Nova and put them on the correct past date?

A: Ring up the older sales as regular sales. Log in with Full Owner access. Go to Management > Accounting > Invoices. Open the invoice. Edit the invoice date to the correct past date.

Q: Can I upload my business logo onto my receipts or invoices?

A: Open Windows Devices and Printers. Right-click the Star TSP100 printer and choose Printer properties. Open TSP Config and add the logo image. Test print the logo, then save it to the printer.

Q: Can Nova delete data from a specific time period for me?

A: Use Nova's invoice tools if you need to bulk delete invoices yourself. Confirm you have owner permission before deleting anything. Back up or export data before making bulk changes.

Q: Can a generic receipt printer work with Nova?

A: Install the printer in Windows. If no manufacturer driver is available, choose Generic as the manufacturer. Select Generic / Text Only. Finish the installation and test printing.

Q: Can deleted invoices be restored?

A: Check whether the invoice was actually deleted or only filtered out of view. If it was deleted, contact support to confirm options. Avoid deleting invoices unless you are sure.

Q: Can the minimum layaway payment be set very low?

A: Open layaway settings. Set the minimum layaway payment percentage to 0%. Save the setting. Enter the desired minimum payment during the layaway sale.

Q: How can I stop employees from doing refunds?

A: Create or edit a custom user role. Disable the Refunds permission for that role. Assign the role to employees who should not refund sales.

Q: How do I add a logo to a receipt printer?

A: Use a supported receipt printer such as Star or Epson for easier logo setup. Locate the bitmap or JPG logo file. Open the printer utility for the receipt printer. Upload or add the image in the utility. Set the uploaded image as the logo at the top of the receipt. Print a test receipt.

Q: How do I attach a file or picture to an invoice?

A: Open the invoice attachment option from the Sales Screen workflow. Choose Attach Files. Select a file or picture from Windows Explorer and click Open. Click Upload. Confirm that the uploaded file appears as an attachment on the invoice.

Q: How do I complete a sale in Classic Nova?

A: Select the products being sold. Click Complete Sale. Choose the salesperson name. Choose the payment type. Click Print to finish the sale and print the receipt.

Q: How do I enable tips for a location?

A: On the Management Screen, choose Locations and then Settings. Open the location. In the Location Information tab, find Receipt Options. Check Allow Tip. Optionally set a default tip. Save the location.

Q: How do I hide the salesperson name on printed receipts?

A: Go to Management > Location > Settings. Edit the location. Open Location Information. Enable the option to hide the sales representative. Save the location settings.

Q: How do I open a new layaway?

A: Select the products being sold. Click Layaway. Choose Add a new layaway. Follow the on-screen prompts. Choose the salesperson and payment type. Print the receipt.

Q: How do I print a gift receipt?

A: Open the invoice reprint option. Choose Yes when asked about printing a gift receipt. Print the receipt.

Q: How do I print a gift receipt?

A: Complete the sale normally. When the home Sales Screen returns, click the Receipts icon at the top of the Sales Screen. Choose Print Latest on the left. When Nova asks whether this is a gift receipt, click Yes.

Q: How do I print two receipts after every sale?

A: Complete the sale from the sales screen. Before printing, check Print 2 receipts. Print the sale.

Q: How do I run a manual refund using Stellar?

A: From the Management Screen, choose Locations and then Settings. Select the location and click Edit Location. Open the Extra Features tab. Click Manual Refund. Sign in with a password or fingerprint device. Full Owner or assigned refund access is required. Enter the refund amount and complete the refund prompts.

Q: How do I set up a Star TSP100 printer for Nova?

A: Plug the Star TSP100 into USB and power, and turn it on. Install the latest Star TSP100 driver. Open Printer properties for the Star TSP100. Set the paper size to 72mm x Receipt in Device Settings. Open Printing Defaults > Advanced and set 72mm x Receipt there too. Run a test print.

Q: How do I take a payment on an existing layaway?

A: Click Layaway. Choose Update Layaway. Enter the layaway code. Enter the payment amount. Choose the salesperson and payment type. Click Complete and print the receipt.

Q: How many files can I attach to an invoice?

A: Complete or open the invoice. Use the attachment option. Attach up to three files.

Q: What access level is needed to delete invoices?

A: Log in as a Full Owner. Open the invoice management area. Delete the invoice only if you are sure it should be removed.

Q: What cables should be connected for a receipt printer and cash drawer?

A: Connect the printer power cable. Connect the printer USB cable to the computer. Connect the cash drawer adapter cable between the cash drawer and the printer.

Q: What filters and actions are available in Invoice Management?

A: Open Accounting - Invoice Management. Choose a From Date and To Date. Use the invoice view selector to view all invoices, invoices under minimum price, or tax-exempt invoices. Use Filter to search the grid. Use Print Selected or Delete Selected if the user's role allows those actions. Use Click here to select data to view to adjust visible columns.

Q: What receipt lookup options are available on the Sales Screen?

A: Open Locate Receipt from the Sales Screen receipts workflow. Use Search for invoice by entering an invoice number and clicking Search. Use Print Latest to reprint the latest receipt. Use Open Latest to open the latest invoice.

Q: What settings should I complete when setting up a location?

A: From the Manager Screen, choose Locations and then Settings. Choose the location and click Edit Location. Complete the Location Information tab, including address, mall name, and tax information that should appear on receipts. Open Location Preferences and check the options that match the location's workflow. Open Merchant Services and choose external credit-card machine or built-in card reader, then enter provider information. Open Extra Features and enable any additional Nova features needed for the location. Save the location.
