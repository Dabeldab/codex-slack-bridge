Q: Can I restrict gift cards by region?

A: Open location or gift card settings. Enable the option to restrict gift cards from regions. Save the setting.

Q: How do I apply a coupon on the Sales Screen?

A: On the Sales Screen, add the eligible items to the shopping cart. Click the Coupons icon. Scan or enter the coupon code, or select the coupon from the list. Confirm that the coupon applies to the matching products in the cart. Complete the sale.

Q: How do I charge an existing gift card?

A: On the Sales Screen, press the Gift Cards icon at the bottom of the screen. Choose Use Existing Gift Card. Enter the unique gift card code and press Locate. After Nova finds the card, choose the amount to charge. Press Charge Card and complete the sale.

Q: How do I create a coupon?

A: On the Manager screen, choose Products and then Coupons. Select an existing coupon or press Add New Coupon. Choose whether the coupon applies to items or the total sale. Enter the coupon name, code, and expiration date. Select the products or categories that qualify. Choose a sales discount, percentage discount, or store credit option and enter the amount. Save the coupon so it can be used on the Sales Screen.

Q: How do I give a discount on the Sales Screen?

A: Option 1: select a product on the Sales Screen and click the Discount icon. Choose percentage or dollar discount and enter the discount value. Option 2: add selected items to the shopping cart and click the Discount icon near the bottom-right of the screen. Choose percentage or dollar discount, enter the value, and press Add. Review the cart to confirm the discount before completing the sale.

Q: How do I use a gift card on a sale?

A: Click the Gift Card button. Choose Use a gift card. Enter the gift card amount to apply. Complete the sale, choose salesperson and payment type, and print.

Q: I created a new product, but it is not showing on the sales screen. What should I do?

A: Go to Management > Inventory. Choose the location. Click Update Inventory. Check Show products not in inventory. Add quantity for the new product. Check Show products in inventory and update inventory.

Q: What does list mode do on the sales screen?

A: Open location settings. Enable list mode if you want product variations and descriptions visible. Save the setting.

Q: Why did my sales screen switch from buttons to list mode?

A: Check how many items are on the sales screen. If there are more than 100 items, Nova may switch to list mode automatically. Adjust the location settings if button mode should be changed.

Q: Why should I use regions in Nova?

A: Group locations into regions when you have multiple locations. Assign users only to the regions they should access. Use regions to keep location data organized and separated.
