Q: Can Nova copy products from another customer's account into mine?

A: Ask the other account owner to authorize the request. Have Nova support confirm authorization. After authorization, support can review whether the products can be added.

Q: How can I sell the same products at different prices in different locations?

A: Go to Management > Products > Price List. Choose the location that needs its own prices. Add a new price list for that location. Save the location-specific prices.

Q: How do I add a picture to a product category?

A: From the Management Screen, choose Products and then Categories. Right-click the category. Choose Add Picture. Click Browse and select a photo from the picture library. Click Save.

Q: How do I set up Nova so tax is included in my product prices?

A: Go to Management > Location > Location Preferences. Select Include tax in product price. Go to Products > Settings. Enable Deduct tax from product price.

Q: What customer-facing actions exist in Product Settings?

A: Open Product Settings from the manager side Products area. Use Active Products or Inactive Products to filter the product list. Use add a new product to create a product. Use Edit Products in Table Mode for grid-style editing. Use Generate Barcodes when barcode labels are needed. Use Save Changes after edits or Cancel Changes to discard them.

Q: What does minimum price restriction do?

A: Open the location or product pricing settings. Enable minimum price restriction to block sales below minimum price. Disable it only if employees should be allowed to sell at any price.
