Q: Can I do an inventory count by category instead of by individual product?

A: Open Inventory Management. Choose View by Category. Run or review the count by category.

Q: How do I create an inventory transfer?

A: Go to Managers Screen > Inventory > Transfers. Click Create a new transfer. Select the source region/location and destination region/location. Choose whether to update now or update when received and counted. Click Load Inventory. Enter quantities in Quantity to Transfer.

Q: How do I create an inventory transfer?

A: From the Manager Screen, choose Inventory and then Transfers. Click the create a new transfer icon in the upper-right corner. Select the source and destination locations. Choose whether the destination updates now or only after receiving and counting inventory. Click Load Inventory or use search to locate items. Enter the quantity to transfer for each item. Continue through the transfer submission prompts.

Q: How do I receive an inventory transfer that was set to update after counting?

A: Open the transfer that was created. Click Open Transfer. Click Receive. Enter the quantity received. Click Receive again to submit.

Q: How do I review a daily inventory count?

A: From the Manager Screen, choose Inventory and then Daily Counts. Choose the From Date and To Date. Click the refresh/load icon. Find the location and count date to review. Click Open Count for item counts or Open Category Count for category counts. If an error exists, use Fix to update inventory to match the counted quantity and save. Report affected items as a loss if needed.

Q: How do I submit an inventory count by category?

A: At the top of the Sales Screen, click the Inventory icon. In Inventory Management, open the View Inventory By Category tab. Enter the count for each category. Click Submit Count.

Q: How do I submit an inventory count by item?

A: At the top of the Sales Screen, click the Inventory icon. In Inventory Management, open the View Inventory By Item tab. Find the Count column on the right side of the grid. Scan or manually enter the counted quantity for each item. Click Submit Count.

Q: How do employees receive an inventory transfer from the sales screen?

A: From the sales screen, click Inventory. Choose Receive Inventory Transfer. Open the transfer. Enter the received quantities and submit.

Q: What does the Fix button do on an inventory count report?

A: Open the submitted inventory count report. Review the employee's count. Click Fix for the item that should be corrected.

Q: What happens when Block sales of items out of stock is enabled?

A: Enable Block sales of items out of stock in settings. When an out-of-stock item is selected, choose whether to transfer, add quantity, or exchange the item.

Q: What tabs and actions are available in Inventory Management?

A: Open Inventory from the location screen. Use View Inventory By Item for item-level counts. Use View Inventory By Category for category counts. Use Update Inventory to add, remove, or send products. Use Report Tester/Damage/Loss to report damaged, lost, tester, or other inventory losses. Use Receive Inventory Transfer to receive transfer records. Use Submit Count, Update Inventory, Report Loss, or new transfer depending on the selected tab.

Q: Where can I see the total value of the inventory in one location?

A: Switch to New Nova if needed. Go to Inventory > Inventory Report. Open Cost of Inventory on Hand.

Q: Why would I hide the status column on inventory count screens?

A: Open the inventory count settings. Enable Hide status column if employees should not see OK or error status while counting. Save the setting.
