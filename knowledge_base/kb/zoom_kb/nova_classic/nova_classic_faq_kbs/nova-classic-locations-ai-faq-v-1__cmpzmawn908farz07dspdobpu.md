Q: How do I add a new location?

A: From the Manager Screen, choose Locations > Settings. Click Add / Activate Locations or add a new location. Owner access is required. If the workflow opens Billing Automation but does not log the owner in automatically, use https://app1.novapointofsale.com/v2/BillingAutomation/Account/Login and sign in manually.

Q: How do I allow sales when no employee is selected?

A: From the Management Screen, choose Locations and then Settings. Select the location and click Edit. Open the Location Preferences tab. Check the option to allow sales with no selected employees. Click Save.

Q: How do I automatically check out employees at a location?

A: From the Management Screen, open Location Settings. Select the location and open the Location Preferences tab. Find the Security section. Check Automatically check out employees at this location after. Enter the automatic checkout time or use the clock icon. Save the location preferences.

Q: How do I close an existing location?

A: From the Manager Screen, choose Locations > Settings. Click Cancel / Freeze locations or the close-location workflow. Full Owner access is required. Select the location or locations to close and click Next. Choose the closing reason and optionally add notes. Confirm the close action and look for the success message. All but the last active sales location can be closed this way. If the customer is closing the last location or full account, route them to the account-closure workflow and have them email billing@novapointofsale.com.

Q: How do I copy location preferences from one location to another?

A: On the Management Screen, choose Locations and then Settings. Open the Location Preferences tab for the source location. Fill out and save the preferences for the source location. Click Edit Location next to the destination location. On the Location Preferences tab, choose the source location in Copy preferences from another location. Click Copy. Review the copied preferences and save if required.

Q: How do I open a new location?

A: From the Manager Screen, choose Locations > Settings. Click Add / Activate Locations or add a new location. Full Owner access is required. Choose the new-location option, enter or edit the location name, choose the region if the company uses regions, choose the needed sales-location setup, review billing/payment information, click Next, agree to the terms and conditions, and confirm that the location opened successfully. If Billing Automation does not log in automatically, send https://app1.novapointofsale.com/v2/BillingAutomation/Account/Login.

Q: How do I reactivate a closed location?

A: Open Open/Close Locations. Click Reactivate Multiple Locations. Select the location to reactivate. Confirm the reactivation.

Q: How do I reactivate a location?

A: From the Manager Screen, choose Locations. Choose Settings. Click Add a new location. Full Owner access is required. Choose Open/Close Locations and then Reactivate Multiple Locations. Select the locations to reactivate and click Next. Confirm that the success message appears.

Q: What is the second tax rate for?

A: Open the location tax settings. Use the second tax rate only if your area requires more than one tax. Save the correct tax rates for the location.

Q: What location-management actions are visible in Nova Classic?

A: Open Location Settings from the manager Locations area. Use Add / Activate Locations for adding or reactivating locations. Use View / Pay / Update my bills for billing tasks. Use Cancel / Freeze locations to stop or pause locations. Use add a new location when creating another location record.

Q: How do I add a new storage location?

A: From Locations > Settings, click Add / Activate Locations or add a new location. Choose New Storage or + Add a new free warehouse location when that option is shown. Storage locations can be added at no extra cost, are unlimited, and remain inactive. Only sales locations are active in Nova.

Q: Why does the billing or add-location page keep returning to login?

A: Billing Automation requires Owner access. Have the owner try Settings > Billing and Payments or Locations > Settings > Add / Activate Locations again. If Nova does not pass them through automatically, send https://app1.novapointofsale.com/v2/BillingAutomation/Account/Login and have them sign in manually.
