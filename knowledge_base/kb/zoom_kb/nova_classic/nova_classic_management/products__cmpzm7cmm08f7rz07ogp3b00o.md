Management Screen > Products
Q: What is the Products section used for?

A: Use Management Screen > Products to add products, edit products, activate or deactivate products, manage product prices, assign products to locations, manage categories, manage wholesalers, create price lists, create coupons, run product reports, compare products, and print labels or price tags.

The main choices under Products are:

Settings
Price Lists
Categories
Wholesalers
Coupons
Coupons Reports
Reports
Compare Products
Labels and Price Tags
Q: How do I open Products?

A:

Go to the Management Screen.
Click Products.
Choose the area you need, such as Settings, Price Lists, Categories, Wholesalers, Coupons, Reports, Compare Products, or Labels and Price Tags.
Q: How do I view active or inactive products?

A:

From the Management Screen, click Products.
Click Settings.
Click Active Products to view active products.
Click Inactive Products to view inactive products.
Use the search box or SEARCH ON option if you need to find a specific product.
Q: How do I add a new product?

A:

Go to Products > Settings.
Click add a new product.
Enter the Product Name.
Enter optional product details such as Description, Size, Color, Barcode 1, Barcode 2, and SKU.
Choose the Wholesaler if the product belongs to a wholesaler.
Choose the Product Category.
Choose a Button Color if the product should have a color marker on the sales screen.
Add or change the Product Photo if needed.
Set the product price in Product Default Price.
Assign the product to the locations where it should be sold.
Click Save, Save and New, or Save and Clone.

The required field is normally Product Name. Other fields help employees search, scan, report, and sell the product correctly.

Q: How do I activate or deactivate a product?

A:

Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
Check Active Product if the product should be active.
Uncheck Active Product if the product should be inactive.
Click Save.

If a product is active but still not visible on the sales screen, check the location assignment and inventory behavior too.

Q: How do I set a product's price?

A:

Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
In Product Default Price, choose Fixed Price or Non-Fixed Price.
Enter Product Cost Price.
Enter Product Markup Price.
Enter Product Minimum Price if employees should not sell below a minimum.
Check Block sales below minimum price if Nova should stop sales below the minimum.
Check Block sales above markup price if employees should not sell above markup.
Click Save.

Pricing options may include Use product-specific tax rate, Deduct Tax Amount from Product Price, and Last Purchase Cost Price.

Q: What is the difference between fixed price and non-fixed price?

A: A Fixed Price product uses the product price as the selling price. A Non-Fixed Price product allows price flexibility based on the rules and permissions in Nova.

To change it:

Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
Choose Fixed Price or Non-Fixed Price in Product Default Price.
Review the cost, markup, and minimum price.
Click Save.
Q: How do I block employees from selling below the minimum price?

A:

Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
Enter the floor price in Product Minimum Price.
Check Block sales below minimum price.
Click Save.
Q: How do I use a product-specific tax rate?

A:

Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
Check Use product-specific tax rate.
Enter the product tax rate if the screen asks for it.
Click Save.

Use this only when the product needs a different tax treatment than the location's normal tax rate.

Q: How do I set product commission?

A:

Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
Find Commission Policy.
Choose Regular Commission, No Commission, or Extra Commission.
If using Extra Commission, enter the extra percentage.
Click Save.
Q: How do I assign a product to a location?

A:

Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
In the location grid, check Check this box if this product is sold here for each location where the product should be sold.
Enter the starting Quantity if needed.
Uncheck a location if the product should not be sold there.
Click Save.
Q: How do I remove a product from only one location?

A:

Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
In the location grid, uncheck the location where the product should not be sold.
Click Save.

This does not have to deactivate the product everywhere. It only removes the product from the selected location.

Q: How do I add or change a barcode?

A:

Go to Products > Settings.

Q: Why is my new product not visible on the sales screen?

Click Save after making changes.

Q: What is different about products and price lists in Classic compared with New Nova?

A: In Nova Classic, product setup and inventory management are separate areas.

Classic paths:

Use Products > Settings for product setup fields such as product name, description, size, color, barcode, SKU, cost price, minimum price, markup price, fixed/non-fixed price, category, tax, picture, active status, and location assignment.
Use Inventory > Management for inventory quantity workflows, counts, testers/losses, transfers, and purchase orders.
Use Products > Price Lists for Classic price lists.
Assign a Classic price list to a location from Products > Price Lists or Locations > Settings > Edit Location > Location Preferences.

New Nova / NextGen paths:

Use Inventory for both product settings and inventory management.
Use the product Name or Action > Edit to open a product.
Use Product Details, Pricing, and Inventory tabs inside the product.
Use Does this product come in different variants (example: color, size)? and Generate Variants for the New Nova product matrix.
Use New Nova Price Lists from the blue menu for bulk price-list percentage/dollar updates.

If the customer is asking about Generate Variants, Custom variant options, or direct blue price edits from the Inventory list, use the New Nova product KB instead of this Classic article.

Q: Why does my item or category coupon require every selected item in the cart?

A: Coupons are strict discount rules. If a coupon is built from selected products, selected categories, or an item/category scope, the required products/categories must be in the cart at the required quantities before the coupon can apply.

For example, if a promotion is built like a BOGO discount and the required products are $50 each, the coupon can be configured so the required products activate a fixed $50 discount. The sale must contain every required activation product/category. If the coupon is set up with 10 required products/categories and the cashier only adds 3 of them to the cart, the coupon will not work.

To troubleshoot:

Go to Products > Coupons.
Open the coupon.
Confirm the coupon is Active.
Confirm the coupon is still inside the Expiration Date or active date range.
Confirm the cashier is using the correct Coupon Code and the correct upper/lowercase if Case Sensitive! is enabled.
Confirm the coupon type/scope matches the products or category on the sale.
Confirm every required product/category is in the cart at the required quantity.
Confirm the discount amount is set correctly.
Save any corrections and test the coupon on the Sales Screen.

If the business needs optional choices, do not use one strict coupon activation list. Use Products > Kits and Bundles when the account has that Classic feature enabled.

Q: Why are random products showing on the sales screen home page?

A: Products can appear unexpectedly on the sales screen when they are not organized into the intended category or when the sales screen display mode needs adjustment.

To fix it:

Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product that is showing in the wrong place.
Confirm the product has the correct Product Category.
Save the product.
If the business has many products, go to Locations > Settings > Edit Location > Location Preferences.
Check Change sales screen to list mode if list mode is better for the location.
Refresh or reopen the sales screen.
Q: Can products be searched by SKU, barcode 2, description, or size on the sales screen?

A: Products can be searched by saved identifiers such as Barcode 1, Barcode 2, and SKU. Some sales-screen searches may also support product details such as description or size depending on the screen and mode.

To make a product easier to find:

Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
Fill in Barcode 1, Barcode 2, and SKU when available.
Fill in Description, Size, and Color when they help employees identify the product.
Click Save.

Q: Why is a new product not visible on the Sales Screen?

A: Check the product's active status, location availability, category, and inventory.

Go to Products > Settings.
Search for the product.
Confirm the product is active.
Confirm the product is assigned to the correct category.
Confirm the product is available for the correct location.
Go to Inventory > Update Inventory for Classic inventory changes, or use New Nova / NextGen > Inventory for current inventory report/export checks.
Confirm the product has quantity at the location if the store hides out-of-stock items.
Return to the Sales Screen and refresh or sign out and back in.

Customers may ask this as "I added inventory but don't see the product," "new item not on sales screen," "product missing," or "item not showing after update."

Q: How do I set up Kits and Bundles?

A: Kits and Bundles let the business group products together for bundled selling or promotions.

Go to Products.
Open Kits & Bundle if the feature is available.
Create a new kit or bundle.
Enter the kit or bundle name.
Add the products or categories that belong in the bundle.
Review the pricing behavior, such as whether the lowest-priced item is affected.
Choose the locations where the kit or bundle should be active.
Save the setup.
Test the bundle on the Sales Screen before training employees to use it.

If the business does not see Kits & Bundle, confirm the user's access level includes that product-management permission and confirm the account has the feature enabled.

Use Kits and Bundles instead of a coupon when the business needs optional products inside the offer. Coupons require the configured activation products/categories to be in the cart. Kits and Bundles are the Classic feature used when the promotion is really a bundle/kit workflow instead of a strict coupon discount.

Q: How do I create a coupon for a category?

A: Use a category coupon when the discount should apply only to products in a certain category.

Go to Products > Coupons.
Create a new coupon.
Enter the coupon name and Coupon Code.
Choose the coupon type or scope that applies to a category.
Select the category.
Enter the fixed discount amount. Category setup coupons cannot use percentage discounts.
Choose the start/end dates and locations if shown.
Check Active when ready.
Click Save.

Test the coupon on a product inside the category and a product outside the category so the business knows the rule is applying correctly.

Q: Can I make a percentage discount coupon for a category?

A: No. Category setup coupons cannot use percentage discounts. This applies to Nova Classic, New Nova / NextGen, and Nova Wave-style coupon answers.

If a customer wants a category coupon:

Go to Products > Coupons.
Create or open the coupon.
Choose the category coupon type or category scope.
Select the category.
Enter a fixed discount amount instead of a percentage.
Save.
Test the coupon on an item inside the category and an item outside the category.

If the customer needs a percentage discount, use a product/item coupon or another supported coupon setup instead of a category setup coupon.

Q: What is Spare?

A: Spare is the amount sold above the product's minimum price. It is used in reports to show the difference between the minimum price and the actual sale price.

Example:

The product minimum price is $50.
The product sells for $70.
The spare amount is $20.

Use reports that include Spare when the customer asks how much extra was sold above minimum price.

Q: What is a Store Credit coupon?

A: A store-credit coupon gives the customer credit instead of only discounting the current sale.

Use it carefully:

Go to Products > Coupons.
Create or open the coupon.
Choose the store-credit coupon type if it is available.
Enter the amount or rule.
Set the active dates and locations.
Click Save.
Test the coupon on a small sale so the store understands whether it creates store credit, discounts the sale, or affects both.

How do I use Edit Products in Table Mode?
Go to Management Screen > Products > Settings.
Use Active Products or Inactive Products to show the correct product list.
Search for the product by name, barcode, category, size, or color.
Check Edit Products in Table Mode.
Edit the visible grid fields that Classic allows from the table, such as cost, minimum price, or markup price.
Use Save Changes if the save option appears.
Use Cancel Changes if the edit was a mistake.
Open the product with Edit if the customer needs full product settings, category assignment, barcodes, photo, color/size, or Save and Clone.

If the customer is trying to change many products at once, table mode is the Classic bulk-edit workflow. New Nova has more bulk tools, including import/export and stronger multi-select actions.

How can I set up a simple BOGO-style coupon?

Example: each product costs $50 and the customer should get one item free when both required items are in the cart.

Go to Management Screen > Products > Coupons.
Open an existing coupon or click add coupon.
Enter the Coupon Name and Coupon Code.
Make the coupon Active.
Turn Visible on if staff should see it from the sale screen.
In Coupon Activation, add the required products or categories.
Set the required quantity for each activation row.
In Coupon Value, use a fixed discount for the free-item value. In this example, enter a fixed discount of 50.
Save the coupon.
Test it on the sale screen by adding all required products/categories to the cart.

Important limitations:

All products/categories added to the coupon must be in the cart.
A category setup coupon cannot do a percentage discount.
If optional item choices are needed, use kits/bundles instead of a coupon.
