CRM Settings

Use CRM Settings to control loyalty rating and required customer fields.

Steps:

Go to Customers > Management.

Configure required CRM fields.
Click Save.

Email Campaign

Use Customers > Email Campaign to send emails to selected CRM customers.

Steps:

Go to Customers > Email Campaign.
Step 1: select customers.
Use Select All or check individual customers.
Step 2: enter Email Campaign Subject.
Add Email Campaign Attachment if needed with Browse.
Write the Email Campaign Body.
Step 3: choose sending method:
Send campaign using my gmail account
Send campaign using a different SMTP server
Enter username/password and SMTP fields if needed.
Click Send Campaign.

Important:

Customers with no email are skipped.
Customers opted out of email campaigns are skipped.

SMTP fields:

Username
Password
SMTP Server
Port
Use SSL
Appointments

Use Appointments to view or add customer appointments.

Steps:

Go to Customers > Management.
Click Appointments.
Select From Date and To Date.
Choose Location, User, and/or Customer.
Double-click a calendar slot to add or edit an appointment.
Fill in customer, user, location, date/time, and recurrence if needed.
Click Save.

Layaways

Use Customers > Layaways to review layaways and configure layaway settings.

Steps to review layaways:

Go to Customers > Layaways.
Select From Date and To Date.
Review layaway list and totals.
Click Open on the layaway if needed.

Steps to set up layaways:

Go to Customers > Layaways.
Click Layaway settings.
Select available locations.
Enter expiration days.
Enter minimum payment required.
Choose whether users can change layaway expiration date.
Enter the layaway disclaimer that prints on the receipt.
Save settings.

Settings:

Expiration days.
Minimum payment required.
% from sale
