Credit Card / Payment Processing Basics

Nova may process credit cards through a gateway and processor.

Customer-friendly explanation:

The gateway is the secure connection that sends card data for authorization.
The processor/acquirer is the financial institution behind the card processing.
The issuing bank approves or declines the transaction.
Settlement/batch moves approved funds to the merchant account.

Gateway/device concepts found in Nova:

Authorize.net
Creditcall
Heartland
Velocity
Stellar
Mercury/Vantiv
EMV devices such as PAX-S300, PAX-SP30, PAX-D210, and Ingenico-iCT220
Delete Data / Data Protection

Use this area with extreme care.

Visible labels:

Reset Account
Please enter your company username
Please enter your company password
Incorrect username and/or password

Customer guidance:

Do not guide a customer through deleting data unless they clearly understand the result.
This operation cannot be undone.
The customer should export/backup needed data before continuing.
If the question is uncertain, route to support instead of giving casual steps.
