Add A New Product

Steps:

Go to Products > Settings.
Click add a new product.
Fill in Product Name.
Add product details such as Description, Size, Color, Barcode 1, Barcode 2, and SKU.
Choose Wholesaler and Product Category if applicable.
Choose Button Color if the product should be visually marked on the sales screen.
Add or change Product Photo if needed.
Set pricing in Product Default Price.
Assign the product to locations in the inventory/location grid.
Click Save, Save and New, or Save and Clone.

How to set a product price:

Open the product.
In Product Default Price, choose whether the product is Fixed Price or Non-Fixed Price.
Enter Product Cost Price.
Enter Product Markup Price.
Enter Product Minimum Price if the product should not sell below a floor price.
Check Block sales below minimum price if employees should be prevented from selling below the minimum.
Check Block sales above markup price if employees should not sell above markup.
Click Save.

Open the product.

Common Customer Questions
Why is my new product not visible on the sales screen?
Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
Make sure Active Product is checked.
Make sure the product is assigned to the location where it should be sold.
Add inventory quantity if the location blocks sales of out-of-stock items.
Click Save.
How do I remove a product from only one location?
Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
In the location grid, uncheck the location where the product should not be sold.
Click Save.
How do I add a barcode?
Go to Products > Settings.
Open the product.
Enter the barcode in Barcode 1 or Barcode 2.
Click Save.
