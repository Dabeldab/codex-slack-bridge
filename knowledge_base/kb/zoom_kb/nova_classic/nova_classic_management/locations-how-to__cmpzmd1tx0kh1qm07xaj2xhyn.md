Add Or Activate Locations

Use Add / Activate Locations when opening a new store/location, activating an inactive location, or adding a warehouse-style location.

Steps:

Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Add / Activate Locations.
In Step 1, choose one of the available actions:
+ Add a new location
+ Activate an inactive location
+ Add a new free warehouse location

Nova Basic
Nova Pro

Add any Additional Comments.
Click Next.
Review billing information and payment method.
Review the order.
Check I have read and understood.
Submit the request.

View, Pay, Or Update Bills

Use View / Pay / Update my bills to review bills, update billing information, and view payment history.

Steps:

Go to Locations > Settings.
Click View / Pay / Update my bills.
Select the billing account you want to view.
Review bills, payment history, or billing information.
Update payment/billing details if needed.

Cancel Or Freeze Locations

Use Cancel / Freeze locations to request closing, freezing, or deactivating locations.

Steps:

Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Cancel / Freeze locations.
Move the location from Active Locations to Locations to cancel / freeze using the arrow button.
Click Next.
Choose the reason:
I am no longer doing business at these locations
These are seasonal locations which I will reopen at a later point
I am freezing these locations and will reopen them soon
My lease has expired at these locations
I sold my location to somebody else
Business is doing great, I am however, unsatisfied with NovaPOS
If asked, enter the planned reopen date.
Add any requested explanation.
Review the request.
Check I have read and understood.
Submit the cancellation/freezing request.

ID
Region
Location
Edit Location: Location Information Tab

Use Location Information for the location's name, region, receipt identity, contact information, taxes, tips, receipt messages, and receipt display options.

Steps:

Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Open the Location Information tab.
Edit the needed fields.
Review the receipt preview.
Click Save.

Tax Rate

Fields/options:

Location Tax Rate
Have "TAX" display as: checkbox plus short tax-name field.
2nd Tax Rate checkbox/section.
Location 2nd Tax Rate
Have "TAX" display as: for second tax name.

How to set the normal tax rate:

Open Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Go to Location Information.
Enter the percentage in Location Tax Rate.
To rename the tax on receipts, check Have "TAX" display as: and type the label, such as GST or VAT.
Click Save.

How to add a second tax rate:

Open Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Go to Location Information.
Expand/check 2nd Tax Rate.
Enter Location 2nd Tax Rate.
If needed, check Have "TAX" display as: and enter the second tax label.
Click Save.

How to change receipt text:

Open Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.

Open Locations > Settings.

How to set automatic checkout:

Open Location Preferences.

Security

Checkboxes:

Enable cash count
Open cash drawer ONLY after a sale has been made

Cash drawer behavior:

If Enable cash count is checked, employees with sales access can perform cash counts.
If Open cash drawer ONLY after a sale has been made is checked, the drawer is blocked from opening for a cash count and opens only after a cash sale.
If both are checked, cash count/open-drawer behavior is more restricted and generally requires higher access.

Steps:

Open Location Preferences.
Go to Security.
Check or uncheck Enable cash count.
Check or uncheck Open cash drawer ONLY after a sale has been made.
Click Save.

Common Customer Questions
How do I edit my store address or phone number?
Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Open Location Information.
Update Address 1, Address 2, City, State, Country, Zip/Postal Code, Primary Phone, or Secondary Phone.
Click Save.
How do I change what prints on receipts?
Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Open Location Information.
Edit Receipt Header Message, Receipt Footer Message, and Refund Policy.
Use the receipt preview to confirm the wording.
Click Save.
How do I stop employees from seeing salaries?
Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Open Location Preferences.
Under Reports, check Block employees from reviewing their salaries.
If needed, also check Block employees from reviewing their salaries from the mobile app.
Click Save.
How do I prevent sales when inventory is zero?
Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Open Location Preferences.
Under Inventory, check Block sale of items out of stock.
Click Save.
How do I make credit card CVV required?
Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Open Merchant Services.
Check Force the user to enter the card CVV information.
Click Save.
How do I allow manual credit-card entry?
Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Open Merchant Services.
Check Allow the user to manually enter credit card information.
Click Save.
How do I block use of an external credit card machine?
Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Open Merchant Services.
Check Block use of an external credit card machine.
Click Save.
How do I set up alerts?
Go to Locations > Settings.
Click Edit Location.
Open Extra Features.
Click Nova Alerts.
Click + add user next to each alert the user should receive.
Choose the delivery options.
Click Save.
