question	answer
What is a coupon in Nova?	A coupon in Nova is an account-wide promotional rule that can apply a Percentage discount, a Fixed discount, or Spare Credit when its condition is met.
Who can access Products -> Coupons?	Only Owner can access Products -> Coupons.
Why are coupons Owner-only?	Coupons are Owner-only because they can override normal product price restrictions, including fixed-price behavior and block-below-minimum behavior.
Can Product Managers access Coupons?	No. Product Managers cannot access Products -> Coupons. Coupons are Owner-only.
Are coupons account-wide or location-based?	Coupons are account-scoped. They are not region-based or location-based.
Can I use coupons in all my regions?	Yes. A coupon created under the account can be used across the account's regions.
How do I create a temporary discount on an item?	Use Products -> Coupons. Coupons are the preferred route when you want a temporary promotion without directly changing stored product pricing.
How do I discount an item without changing its price?	Use Products -> Coupons. Coupons let you create temporary promotional behavior without directly changing the product's stored pricing.
How do I create specials?	Go to Back Office -> Products -> Coupons and create a coupon with the desired condition and discount type.
How do I create special campaigns?	Use Products -> Coupons. Create a coupon, set its status and activation condition, choose one discount type, then save it.
How can I update my pricing for a special?	There are two valid routes. Use Products -> Coupons for a temporary promotion without directly changing stored product pricing. Use Products -> Price Lists when you want an alternate pricing structure assigned to one or more locations.
Should I use a coupon or a price list for a temporary promotion?	Use Coupons when you want a temporary promotion without directly changing stored product pricing. Use Price Lists when you want alternate pricing assigned to one or more locations.
What is the difference between a coupon and a price list?	Coupons create temporary promotional rules at checkout. Price Lists create or apply alternate pricing structures. Coupons are account-scoped promotional rules, while Price Lists are alternate pricing schemes that can be assigned to locations.
Can coupons override minimum price or fixed-price restrictions?	Yes. Coupons can override normal product price restrictions.
What does Visible On Sale Screen mean?	It controls whether the coupon is visible to the salesperson on the sales screen.
Can a coupon be automatic and hidden?	Yes. A coupon can be configured to apply automatically and not be visible on the sales screen.
What does Apply automatically mean?	It means the coupon can apply automatically when its condition is met.
What does Prompt user before applying mean?	If Prompt user before applying is On, the user gets a prompt suggesting the coupon and may choose not to apply it. If it is Off, the coupon is added immediately.
What are the coupon status options?	The status options are Always Active, Active Between Date Range, and Not Active.
What does Active Between Date Range mean?	It means the coupon is active only during the configured date range and time window.
What types of coupons can I create?	Nova coupons can be structured three main ways: subtotal-based, item-count-based, or specific-item-based.
What discount types are available in coupons?	The discount types are Percentage, Fixed, and Spare Credit.
What conditions can activate a coupon?	A coupon can activate when the sale subtotal is larger than a threshold, when the number of items in the cart is greater than a threshold, or when there are specific items in the cart.
What is the difference between subtotal, item-count, and specific-item conditions?	Subtotal checks the whole sale subtotal. Item-count checks the total number of items in the cart. Specific-item conditions use selected products, categories, or families in the cart.
Does a subtotal coupon affect the whole sale?	Yes. A subtotal-based coupon affects the whole sale.
Does an item-count coupon affect the whole sale?	Yes. An item-count coupon affects the whole sale.
Does a product coupon affect only the selected products?	Yes. For specific-item coupons using Product, Percentage and Fixed apply only to the qualifying selected products. Spare Credit affects the whole sale.
Does a category coupon affect only the matching category items?	Yes. For specific-item coupons using Category, Percentage and Fixed apply only to the qualifying selected category items. Spare Credit affects the whole sale.
Does a family coupon affect only the matching family items?	Yes. For specific-item coupons using Family, Percentage and Fixed apply only to the qualifying selected family items. Spare Credit affects the whole sale.
What does Product mean in the specific-items condition?	It means one or more specific individual products can be selected for the coupon condition.
What does Category mean in the specific-items condition?	It means one or more categories can be selected. The coupon becomes valid when the required number of items in the cart comes from any of the selected categories.
Can I select more than one category?	Yes. More than one category can be selected.
What does Family mean in a coupon?	Family means the father product and all of its variants together.
Can I select more than one family?	Yes. One or more families can be selected.
What does Spare Credit mean in a coupon?	In this coupon context, Spare Credit gives spare value. It does not simply act like a normal direct discount.
Is Spare Credit the same as spare?	Yes. In this coupon context, Spare Credit means spare value.
Can I combine percentage and fixed on one coupon?	No. A coupon can use one discount type only.
Can I combine coupon types on one coupon?	No. Percentage, Fixed, and Spare Credit are mutually exclusive. One coupon can use only one discount type.
Can I have more than one condition on one coupon?	No. One coupon supports one condition only.
Does Coupon Code have to be unique?	Yes. Coupon Code must be unique.
Can Coupon Name be more descriptive than Coupon Code?	Yes. Coupon Name can be descriptive for management, while Coupon Code can be shortened for customer-facing use or marketing use.
What happens when I save a coupon?	Saving the coupon opens the print-template dialog.
Do I have to print the coupon after saving?	No. Printing is optional. You can click Cancel to exit the print dialog.
Can I edit a coupon later?	Yes. Hover over the coupon card and click Edit.
Can I remove a coupon?	Yes. Hover over the coupon card and click Remove.
What happens if I remove a coupon?	Remove deletes the coupon.
Can coupons stack?	Yes. Coupons can stack if all coupon conditions remain true together.
Why did one coupon stop applying after another coupon was added?	If one coupon changes the sale in a way that breaks another coupon's condition, the second coupon can no longer apply.
Why should I be careful with fixed discount coupons?	A fixed discount can create a $0 sale if the discount amount matches or exceeds the effective product price.
Why should I be careful with Spare Credit coupons?	Too much Spare Credit may let employees add items at no customer charge using the spare.
Are blanket category coupons risky?	Yes. Blanket coupons across categories, families, or multiple items are riskier because they may affect products with different pricing structures.
What should I check before creating a coupon?	Review the target products' pricing structure, the condition, the discount type, and whether the coupon is broad across categories, families, or multiple items.
Can I make a coupon available only in one store?	No. Coupons are account-scoped and cannot be limited to one specific store or location. For a store-only promo, use Products -> Price Lists.
How do I run a store-only promo?	Use Products -> Price Lists, not Coupons. Price Lists are the documented way to apply alternate pricing to one location only.
