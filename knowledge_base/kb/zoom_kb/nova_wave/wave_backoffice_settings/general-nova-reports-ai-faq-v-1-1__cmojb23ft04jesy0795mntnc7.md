Q: What is the Quick Filter Bar in Nova reports?

A: The Quick Filter Bar is the shared top filter area used by most Back Office reports to control report scope, quick date selection, custom date selection, Region filtering, and location filtering.

Q: What does the Quick Filter Bar do in Nova reports?

A: The Quick Filter Bar controls reporting scope, quick date selection, custom date selection, Region filtering, and location filtering.

Q: Can I set a custom date range in the Quick Filter Bar in Nova?

A: Yes. The Quick Filter Bar supports custom From and To dates.

Q: What is the Dashboard in Nova Back Office?

A: Dashboard is the quick overview screen in Back Office that shows revenue, cost of goods sold, total invoices, payment method breakdown, recent invoices, top performance views, and who is at work right now.

Q: Where is the Dashboard in Nova Back Office?

A: Go to Back Office -> Dashboard.

Q: Who can access the Dashboard in Nova Back Office?

A: Dashboard access is documented for Owner or Full Owner, Partial Owner, and Regional Manager without profit visibility.

Q: Can a Partial Owner see profit on the Dashboard in Nova?

A: Yes. Partial Owner dashboard access is documented with profit visibility.

Q: How do I view my business revenue in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Dashboard and review the Total Revenue tile for the selected scope and date range.

Q: How do I open the full invoice list from the Dashboard in Nova?

A: Use the Invoices widget and click View All to open Back Office -> Accounting -> Invoices.

Q: How do I force check out an employee in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Dashboard, look at the Who is at Work section, and use the force check out button next to the checked-in employee.

Q: My employee forgot to check out. How do I check them out from my end in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Dashboard, open the Who is at Work section, and click the force check out button next to that employee.

Q: Can I print the Dashboard in Nova?

A: No. Dashboard is view only and cannot be printed from this screen.

Q: What is Compare Products in Nova Back Office?

A: Compare Products is a quick-glance product performance report in Back Office that shows top five best profit products, top five most sold products by quantity, bottom five least sold products, and a sold-products list for the selected report period.

Q: Where is Compare Products in Nova Back Office?

A: Go to Back Office -> Products -> Compare Products.

Q: Who can access Compare Products in Nova?

A: Compare Products access is documented for Owner or Full Owner, Partial Owner, and Product Manager.

Q: Can I download the Compare Products report in Nova?

A: Yes. Open Back Office -> Products -> Compare Products and click Download in Excel.

Q: What is Inventory Report in Nova Back Office?

A: Inventory Report is a Back Office report that shows inventory statistics, sold-versus-not-sold visibility for the selected time period, and the current state of inventory quantities.

Q: Where is Inventory Report in Nova Back Office?

A: Go to Back Office -> Inventory -> Report.

Q: Who can access Inventory Report in Nova?

A: Inventory Report access is documented for Owner or Full Owner, Partial Owner, and Inventory Manager.

Q: How do I download my inventory from the inventory report in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Inventory -> Report and use the report's download or export function.

Q: Can I print my inventory from the inventory report in Nova?

A: Yes. Inventory Report supports printing from this screen.

Q: Can I print multiple inventories together from Inventory Report in Nova?

A: Yes. Inventory Report can be used to print multiple inventories together.

Q: Can I print only one selected inventory from Inventory Report in Nova?

A: Yes. You can filter and select one inventory and print only that inventory.

Q: How do I see whether an item sold in a selected time period in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Inventory -> Report. The report shows whether a product sold in the selected timeframe.

Q: What extra details appear in the Inventory Report Excel export in Nova?

A: The Excel export shows more product detail than the on-screen report, including Barcode 2 and SKU.

Q: What is Inventory History in Nova Back Office?

A: Inventory History is the Back Office screen used to review saved inventory history snapshots.

Q: Where is Inventory History in Nova Back Office?

A: Go to Back Office -> Inventory -> History.

Q: Who can access Inventory History in Nova?

A: Inventory History access is documented for Owner or Full Owner, Partial Owner, and Inventory Manager.

Q: How do I access my inventory history in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Inventory -> History.

Q: How do I know what my inventory was like in a given timeframe in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Inventory -> History. This screen shows available historical inventory snapshots.

Q: Can I print inventory history in Nova?

A: Yes. You can open a history entry and print it.

Q: Can I download inventory history in Nova?

A: Yes. You can open a history entry and download it.

Q: Why do I not have inventory history from three years ago in Nova?

A: Older inventory history may no longer exist because Inventory History is limited by retained snapshots.

Q: Why can I not find inventory history for a specific date in Nova?

A: Visibility depends on the captured snapshot schedule and retention window. If no retained snapshot exists for that date, it will not appear.

Q: Does Inventory History show every minute of inventory change in Nova?

A: No. Inventory History shows snapshots, not continuous minute-by-minute historical reconstruction.

Q: If inventory history is saved once a week, can I see daily inventory history in Nova?

A: No. If snapshots were captured once a week, visibility is limited to those weekly snapshots.

Q: Why is my inventory history blank or missing in Nova?

A: Inventory History only shows snapshots that were enabled and retained for that location. If your inventory history is blank or missing, first verify the inventory history setting in Back Office -> Locations -> Settings. Then verify that history was configured correctly for that location and that the date you want falls within the available retained snapshots. Inventory History does not show a continuous minute-by-minute history.

Q: What is Layaways in Nova Back Office?

A: Layaways is the Back Office screen used to review and locate layaway invoices, see layaway status, see the layaway code, and open layaway invoices for basic follow-up actions.

Q: Where is Layaways in Nova Back Office?

A: Go to Back Office -> Customers -> Layaways.

Q: Who can access Layaways in Nova?

A: Layaways access is documented for Owner or Full Owner and Partial Owner.

Q: How do I locate a layaway in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Customers -> Layaways. This is one of the main ways to locate a layaway.

Q: How do I reprint a layaway in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Customers -> Layaways, locate the layaway, open the layaway invoice, and then print it.

Q: Where can I see the status of a layaway in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Customers -> Layaways. The layaway status is visible in the layaway list.

Q: Where can I find the layaway code in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Customers -> Layaways. The layaway code is visible on this screen.

Q: Is the layaway code exposed anywhere else in Nova?

A: The layaway code is clearly visible on the Layaways screen. Otherwise, it is generally not exposed unless the layaway is printed.

Q: Can I open a layaway invoice from the Layaways screen in Nova?

A: Yes. You can open the layaway invoice from the Layaways screen.

Q: What basic actions are documented from a layaway invoice in this reports knowledge base?

A: In this reports knowledge base, the documented basic actions are print and email.

Q: Can I download layaways to Excel in Nova?

A: Yes. The Layaways screen can be downloaded to Excel.

Q: Is Customers -> Layaways the only way to find a layaway in Nova?

A: No. Another way is through the customer record, because layaways always have customers attached.

Q: How else can I locate a layaway in Nova?

A: You can also locate the layaway through the customer because layaways are always attached to customers.

Q: Why should I use the customer path to find a layaway in Nova?

A: Use the customer path when you are locating the layaway through the attached customer and want to review or print the customer's purchase history.

Q: What is Invoice Management in Nova Back Office?

A: Invoice Management is the Back Office accounting report screen used to review invoice reporting for the selected filters.

Q: Where is Invoice Management in Nova Back Office?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Invoices. The page title is Invoice Management.

Q: Who can access Invoice Management in Nova?

A: Invoice Management access is documented for Owner or Full Owner and Partial Owner.

Q: How do I locate an invoice in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Invoices. Confirm the page title says Invoice Management, set Scope and Date, then search by invoice id or use the Invoice Type filter. Review Id, Type, Timestamp, Location, Invoice Total, and Actions.

Q: Where do I find my invoices in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Invoices. Use Date presets such as Today, Past 7 days, This Month, Last Month, or custom From and To dates to find the correct invoice period.

Q: What statistics are shown on Invoice Management in Nova?

A: Invoice Management shows Total Invoices, Total Gross, Total Spare, Total Tax, and Total Net for the selected filters.

Q: Can I search by invoice id in Invoice Management in Nova?

A: Yes. Invoice Management includes search by invoice id.

Q: Can I filter by invoice type in Invoice Management in Nova?

A: Yes. Invoice Management includes an Invoice Type filter.

Q: What invoice type options are in Invoice Management in Nova?

A: The live Invoice Type options are Unknown, Regular, Tax Exempt, Refund, Layaway, and Exchange.

Q: How do I find refund invoices in Invoice Management in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Invoices, set the date range, and set Invoice Type to Refund. You can also use Back Office -> Accounting -> Refunds when you need the original Invoice Id#, Refund Id, Refund Timestamp, or Performed By details.

Q: Can I export invoices to Excel in Nova?

A: Yes. Invoice Management can be exported to Excel.

Q: Can I open an invoice from Invoice Management in Nova?

A: Yes. You can open an invoice from the Invoice Management screen.

Q: What is the difference between Open Invoice and Print Gift Card in Nova?

A: Open Invoice opens the invoice, but printing the invoice does not expose the gift card code. Print Gift Card prints the gift card itself and exposes the code.

Q: What does the Print Gift Card dialog say in Nova?

A: The Print Gift Card dialog says "Select one of the given gift card templates to proceed" and includes PRINT and CANCEL.

Q: Why is printing a gift card considered sensitive in Nova?

A: A gift card represents money, so the gift card itself is considered sensitive. Printing the gift card exposes the code.

Q: What is a profit loss report in Nova?

A: Profit Loss Report is the Back Office report that shows revenue, taxes, expenses, payroll, inventory losses, and other cost components to estimate profit for the selected period.

Q: How does the profit loss report work in Nova?

A: The Profit Loss Report starts from revenue and then subtracts taxes and the main business costs, such as expenses, cost of goods sold, payroll, and inventory losses, to show the estimated profit for the selected period.

Q: What is Profit Loss Report in Nova Back Office?

A: Profit Loss Report is the Back Office report screen used to review income, expenses, and estimated profit for the selected filters.

Q: Where is Profit Loss Report in Nova Back Office?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Profit Loss Report.

Q: Who can access Profit Loss Report in Nova?

A: Profit Loss Report access is documented for Owner or Full Owner and Partial Owner.

Q: Where can I find total net revenue in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Profit Loss Report.

Q: Where can I find tax revenue in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Profit Loss Report.

Q: Where can I find gross revenue in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Profit Loss Report.

Q: Where can I find estimated profit in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Profit Loss Report.

Q: What sections are on the Profit Loss Report in Nova?

A: Profit Loss Report includes Total Net Revenue, Total Tax Revenue, Total Gross Revenue, Profit Information, Expense Information, Sold Products, Sales By Location, Sales By Employees, Cost of Goods Sold, Employees Salary, Expense By Category, and Inventory Loss Reasons.

Q: What columns are shown in Profit Loss Report sections in Nova?

A: Sold Products shows Product, Quantity, Net, Tax, and Gross. Sales By Location shows Location, Net, Tax, and Total. Sales By Employees shows Employee, Location, and Total Sale. Cost of Goods Sold shows Name & Variants and Cost. Employees Salary shows Employee, Location, and Salary. Expense By Category shows Category and Amount. Inventory Loss Reasons shows Reason, Quantity, and Cost.

Q: How is estimated profit determined in Nova's Profit Loss Report?

A: On the Profit Loss Report, estimated profit is explained as gross revenue minus tax, minus expenses, minus cost of goods sold, minus payroll cost, minus inventory losses.

Q: Where do I find the statistics shown on the Profit Loss Report screen in Nova?

A: Those statistics are found in Back Office -> Accounting -> Profit Loss Report.

Q: What is Expenses Management in Nova Back Office?

A: Expenses Management is the Back Office Accounting screen used to review, export, add, and edit expenses.

Q: Where is Expenses Management in Nova Back Office?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Expenses.

Q: What labels and buttons are on Expenses Management in Nova?

A: Expenses Management includes Date, Today, Past 7 days, This Month, Last Month, Region, From, To, Total Expenses, EDIT CATEGORIES, ADD NEW EXPENSE, Region Expenses, and Download in Excel.

Q: What columns are shown in Region Expenses in Nova?

A: Region Expenses shows Id, Category Name & Time, Employee Name, Details, Total Cost, and Actions.

Q: How do I add a new expense in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Expenses and click ADD NEW EXPENSE. The Create an Expense dialog includes Amount, Category, Convert to Recurring Expense, Date, Details, ADD, and CANCEL.

Q: How do I edit expense categories in Nova?

A: Go to Back Office -> Accounting -> Expenses and click EDIT CATEGORIES. The Expenses Categories dialog includes Add New Category, Category Name, Actions, ADD, and CANCEL.

Q: How do expenses connect to Profit Loss Report in Nova?

A: Expenses entered in Accounting -> Expenses are part of the expense side of Accounting -> Profit Loss Report. Use Expenses to add or manage expense records, and use Profit Loss Report to review how expenses affect profitability.

Q: What should I use in Nova if I need a deeper trail for one inventory product?

A: Use Inventory View Changes for a deeper product-level paper trail.

Q: How do I see what happened to a single product in inventory in Nova?

A: Use Inventory View Changes. It can be filtered to a specific product and shows what happened to that product over time.

Q: What can Inventory View Changes show in Nova?

A: Inventory View Changes can show when a specific product was added to inventory, sold, transferred, or reported as lost or damaged.

Q: What is the difference between Inventory Report and Inventory View Changes in Nova?

A: Inventory Report is the broader inventory report. Inventory View Changes is the deeper product-level trace report for a single inventory item.
