How do I add a new product?	Go to Back Office > Products > Settings and click Add New Product. To save a product, complete Product Name, at least one Category, and Product Suggested Price. Nova also recommends filling out as much product information as possible, including Vendor, Description, SKU, Barcode 1, and Barcode 2, because that helps with reporting, retrieval, organization, and purchase-order workflows. If the product should be sellable in a store, also add it to the correct location inventory after creating it.
How do I create a new product in Nova?	Open Back Office > Products > Settings, click Add New Product, complete Product Name, at least one Category, and Product Suggested Price, then save with SUBMIT, SUBMIT & CLONE, or SUBMIT & NEW. Nova also recommends completing the optional product details when available. If the product should be sellable in a store, also add it to the correct location inventory after creating it.
What is the minimum required to create a product?	The minimum required fields are Product Name, at least one Category, and Product Suggested Price.
What do you recommend filling out when creating a product?	Nova recommends completing as much product information as possible. Add Vendor when known, add Description, and enter SKU, Barcode 1, and Barcode 2 when available. This improves retrieval, reporting, organization, and purchase-order workflows.
Why should I add a vendor to the product?	Adding a vendor helps with product organization, reporting, and purchase-order workflows.
Do I need to add the product to inventory after creating it?	Yes, if you want it to appear on the Storefront or sales screen for a location. Creating the product alone does not automatically make it sellable at a store.
Is that all I need to fill out when creating a product?	No. Those are only the minimum required fields to save the product. Nova recommends filling out as much product information as possible, especially Vendor, Description, SKU, Barcode 1, and Barcode 2, because that helps with reporting, retrieval, organization, and purchase-order workflows.
What else should I fill out when I create a product?	After the minimum required fields, Nova recommends completing the optional product details when available, especially Vendor, Description, SKU, Barcode 1, and Barcode 2. That makes the product easier to find later and more useful in reporting and organization.
How do I delete a product?	Nova does not delete a product through Products Settings. To remove a product from every location / sales screen, go to Back Office > Products > Settings, open the product, and turn Active Product Off, then save. This is account-level and removes the product from all locations until it is reactivated.
How do I remove a product?	If you want to remove the product from every location / sales screen, open the product in Back Office > Products > Settings and turn Active Product Off, then save. If you only want to remove it from a specific region, most regions, or selected locations, remove it from each individual location inventory instead.
How do I take a product off the screen?	Open the product in Back Office > Products > Settings, find Active Product, turn it Off, and save. That makes the product inactive account-wide, so it no longer appears for sale at any location until it is reactivated.
How do I remove products from most regions?	Nova Wave does not have a direct region-level remove-product action. Region is used to choose/filter locations. To remove products from most regions, go to Back Office > Inventory > Settings, select the target Region, select the first Location in that region, choose View Inventory By Item or View Inventory By Category, find the products, then use the Remove Product column/action for each product. Click Remove Product. In the confirmation dialog that asks Do you want to remove this product from inventory?, click REMOVE. Once you click REMOVE, the product is removed from that location. Repeat the same removal steps for each individual location in that region and for each additional region as needed. For many products, remove the target products for one location first, then move to the next location. No daily count submission step is required for product removal.
How do I remove products in bulk from a specific region?	Do the bulk inventory removal one location at a time. Go to Back Office > Inventory > Settings, select the Region, select one Location, use View Inventory By Item or View Inventory By Category, find the target products, then use the Remove Product column/action. Click Remove Product. In the confirmation dialog that asks Do you want to remove this product from inventory?, click REMOVE. Once you click REMOVE, the product is removed from that location. Repeat for every other location in that region. Do not use Active Product unless the product should be removed from all locations / sales screens. No daily count submission step is required for product removal.
Should I make a product inactive to remove it from a region?	No, not for one region or selected locations. Active Product is account-level and removes the product from all locations / sales screens. For a specific region, remove the product from each individual location inventory in that region.
I created a product but I do not see it on the sales screen. What should I check?	The most common causes are that Active Product is Off, the product was not added to the correct location inventory, or you are checking the wrong location context. Check those in that order first.
What is ranking in products?	In Nova, Ranking (Rank) is an optional ordering setting that controls where the product appears on the Storefront or sales screen. If you do not set a rank, products display alphabetically by default. If you do set a rank, products are ordered by rank in this sequence: Blue, Yellow, Red, Green, Gray, White, Orange.
Can numbers be used to sort products on the Storefront?	Yes. If no Rank is selected, products sort alphabetically by product name, so leading numbers such as 01, 02, 03 can be used to influence the Storefront or sales-screen order. The numbers become part of the product name, so use this only if that display is acceptable.
What is SKU and Barcode 1 and Barcode 2?	SKU is an internal identifier that does not print on the receipt and is not visible on Storefront. Barcode 1 is visible on Storefront and prints on the receipt. Barcode 2 does not print on the receipt and is not visible on Storefront, but it can still retrieve the product when scanned.
Can I search products by SKU or barcode in the backend?	Yes. SKU, Barcode 1, and Barcode 2 can all be used to search or retrieve products in management areas such as Products > Settings and inventory-related workflows, and some reports can also surface these fields.
Do barcodes have to be unique?	No. Nova does not enforce barcode uniqueness. Users should still treat unique barcodes as a best practice.
Which price setup should I use if my products are always one price?	Recommended setup is to set Product Suggested Price to the normal selling price and set Is Negotiable to Off. Product Minimum Price is mainly relevant when negotiation is allowed.
What is product commission?	Product commission is the commission behavior assigned at the product level through Commission Policy. It can block commission, follow the user's normal commission settings, or add Extra Commission based on net sold price before tax and after discounts. Be careful with this setting because it can override, increase, or reduce payouts, and incorrect setup can create overpayment, underpayment, disputes, or margin loss.
How do I add product commission?	Go to Back Office > Products > Settings, open the product or click Add New Product, then in Product Information set Commission Policy. If you choose Extra Commission, enter the value in Extra Commission(%). Allowed values are integers from -100 to 100. Be careful with this setting because it can stack with user commission, reduce payouts if negative, and materially change payroll outcomes.
How do I add extra commission to a product?	Go to Back Office > Products > Settings, open the product, set Commission Policy to Extra Commission, enter the Extra Commission(%) value, then save the product. Extra Commission is calculated from the net sold price before tax and after discounts. Be careful because this can create overpayment, underpayment, disputes, or margin loss if configured incorrectly.
What is the difference between None, Regular, and Extra Commission?	None pays 0 commission and overrides user commission. Regular follows the user's normal commission settings. Extra Commission adds extra commission on top of user-level commission, if any. Be careful because this setting can increase or reduce payouts.
Should I be careful when setting product commission?	Yes. Product commission is a sensitive payroll-affecting setting. It can override, increase, or reduce payouts, and incorrect setup can create unexpected overpayment, underpayment, disputes, or margin loss.
I want to add product commission. How do I do it?	Go to Back Office > Products > Settings, open the product or click Add New Product, then in Product Information choose Commission Policy. If you use Extra Commission, enter the Extra Commission(%) value and save. Important: product commission is a sensitive payroll-affecting setting. It can stack with user commission, reduce payouts if negative, and create overpayment or underpayment if configured incorrectly.
I want to add extra commission. What should I be careful about?	Extra Commission adds a product-level amount on top of user commission, if any. It is calculated from the net sold price before tax and after discounts. Be careful because it can materially change payouts. If set incorrectly, it can create overpayment, underpayment, disputes, or margin loss.
I need this item to have extra commission. How do I set it?	Go to Back Office > Products > Settings, open the product, set Commission Policy to Extra Commission, enter the Extra Commission(%) value, and save. Important: Extra Commission increases payouts on that product. Negative values reduce payouts. Review the setting carefully before saving.
How do I put commission on a product?	Go to Back Office > Products > Settings, open the product, then in Product Information set Commission Policy. If you choose Extra Commission, enter the Extra Commission(%) value and save. Be careful with this setting because it can increase, reduce, or override expected payouts.
Can Extra Commission increase payouts?	Yes. Extra Commission adds a product-level amount on top of user commission, if any. Because of that, it can increase payouts and should be reviewed carefully before saving.
Can negative Extra Commission reduce payouts?	Yes. Negative Extra Commission reduces payouts. If configured incorrectly, it can create underpayment, disputes, or unexpected payroll outcomes.
How do I create product variations?	Either clone the base product and change Size or Color for each copy, or open the product, go to Variable Product Details, add option families and values, create options, configure the first variant, optionally use Copy for all, and save.
How do I create colors or sizes for one product?	Use Product Variations. For a few versions, use the manual clone method. For scalable setup, use Variable Product Details to create color or size option values.
What is the difference between the clone method and Variable Product Details?	The clone method is simpler for a small number of variants. Variable Product Details is the scalable matrix method for building many combinations more efficiently.
