How do I install Nova Wave on a new computer?	Step 1: Download the installer from https://pos.novapointofsale.com/novapos/beta-nova-wave/setup.exe (or from your welcome email or the Nova website footer). Step 2: Run the setup.exe file and follow the Windows prompts. Step 3: Launch Nova from the desktop shortcut. Step 4: Click ADD NEW ACCOUNT (round + pill button, top of right panel). Step 5: Enter your Company Username in the Company Name field and your Company Password in the Access Key field (include = symbols exactly). Step 6: Click Save. Step 7: Select Region and Location, click Login, then sign in with your personal User Name and Password.	Installation	high
How do I reinstall Nova Wave on a replacement computer?	Download the installer again from https://pos.novapointofsale.com/novapos/beta-nova-wave/setup.exe (or from the Nova website footer or your welcome email). Run it, then click ADD NEW ACCOUNT and re-enter your Company Username and Company Password from your welcome email. You do not need a new account — just re-link the same account to the new computer.	Installation	high

After launching Nova Wave for the first time, click the ADD NEW ACCOUNT pill button (round, red-outlined, + icon) at the top of the right-side login panel. Enter your Company Username in the Company Name field and your Company Password in the Access Key field. Include = symbols exactly. Click Save. A green "Account added successfully"" toast confirms success."	Add Account	high
My Access Key isn't being accepted in Nova Wave.	Four common causes: (1) The = symbols at the start or end were stripped — copy the full Access Key from the welcome email including all = characters. (2) Extra spaces from copying — click the eye icon to view what was entered, remove any whitespace. (3) Fields swapped — Company Username goes in Company Name, Company Password goes in Access Key. (4) Personal daily-login password used instead of the Company Password from the email. If still failing, call support at 1-800-370-6682 Option 3.	Add Account	high

Why do the = signs matter in the Access Key?	The = symbols are part of the Access Key value (it uses base64 encoding, which naturally includes = padding). Removing them makes the key invalid. Copy the entire value from the welcome email exactly as shown — including any = at the start, end, or both.	Credentials	medium
I installed Nova Wave but can't sign in after it said "Account added successfully""."	Adding the account is a one-time install step — signing in is a separate daily step. After the success message, select your Region and Location from the dropdowns on the login screen, click Login, then pick your personal User Name from the dropdown and enter your personal Password in the Sign In dialog.	Daily Sign-In	medium

The Sign In dialog ("Nice to see you again"") opens — pick your User Name from the dropdown and enter your personal Password. Click Sign In. Your personal Password is the one set on your user record in Back Office → Users → Settings, not the Company Password from the welcome email."	Daily Sign-In	medium
