The smaller outlined circle (‹) at the bottom-left of the right panel goes back one step in the checkout flow only (Customer → Payment → Sales Team). Use the top-left arrow to leave checkout and the bottom-left to navigate backwards within checkout.	Checkout Navigation	high
If I click the back arrow in checkout will it cancel my sale?	The top-left red filled circle arrow exits the checkout screen and returns you to the product catalog. Your cart items may still be present. The bottom-left outlined circle arrow only goes back one step in the checkout flow without cancelling anything. If you are unsure, use the bottom-left arrow to navigate backwards safely.	Checkout Navigation	high
How do I enter a cash amount on the payment screen?	On the Payment tab with Cash selected, click a denomination button (1, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100) to add bill values quickly. For a custom amount, click the Custom button or tap the Charged Amount field directly — this opens a numeric keypad where you can type any amount. Once the total entered meets or exceeds the sale total, click Confirm Payment.	Payments	high
Why is the credit card payment not working even though I keep trying?	If you see a 'Payment gateway is not configured' banner, the integrated card reader software has not been set up for this location. Clicking Confirm Payment will fail each time with a notification but nothing will process. Contact your manager to configure the payment gateway in the Back Office. In the meantime, if you have a standalone card terminal, use the External payment method instead of Credit Card.	Payment Errors	high
What is the External payment method and when do I use it?	External is the payment method for locations that process credit cards through a standalone card terminal that is not integrated with Nova. If your store has a physical card machine that is not connected to Nova's software, use External — not Credit Card — to record those transactions. Note: External transactions currently appear as Cash in reporting, so notify your manager if accurate payment method tracking is required.	Payments	high
Why does my cash drawer not match my reports at end of day?	If your location uses a standalone card terminal and cashiers have been recording those payments as External, be aware that External transactions currently record as Cash in Nova's reports. This will cause your cash total in reports to be higher than your actual physical cash. Until this is corrected, keep manual records of external card transactions separately to reconcile against the cash report.	Payments	high
Can I search for a gift card by number during checkout?	Gift cards can be searched during checkout but for security reasons you should only apply a gift card that the customer physically presents to you with their own code. Do not search for or browse gift cards — only apply the specific code the customer provides. If the customer cannot provide their gift card code, do not apply it. Contact your manager if a customer claims to have a gift card but cannot produce the code.	Payments	high
A customer says they have a gift card but does not have the physical card — what do I do?	Do not search for gift cards by browsing the system. The customer must provide their specific gift card code. If they do not have it, they cannot use it at this time. Direct them to check their email (gift card codes may have been emailed when the card was purchased) or contact your manager for assistance. Searching for and applying gift cards that customers cannot identify by code is a security risk.	Payments	high
What does it mean when the toolbar icons look grey?	The grey appearance of the toolbar icons in the top-right of the catalog screen is just their visual style — they are functional and clickable. Hover over any icon to see its tooltip label. The icons provide access to settings, security lock, time/clock functions, receipts, and your user profile.	Navigation	low
What is the Freeze Sale feature?	Freeze Sale allows a cashier to pause (freeze) an active transaction without losing the items in the cart. This lets the cashier serve another customer or step away temporarily, then return to complete the original sale. Access it from the toolbar icon on the catalog screen (snowflake icon, labeled Freeze Sale).	Navigation	medium
