Hardware — General (Connectivity, Performance, Mac) — FAQ
Q: Nova says I'm offline. What do I do?

A: Click Reload or Go Online if that button appears in Nova. If that doesn't work, restart Nova and log back in. If Nova still can't connect, open a web browser and try loading any website to confirm the internet itself is up. Once the internet is working and websites load, Nova will reconnect.

Q: Can I still ring up sales when Nova is offline?

A: If the store needs to keep ringing sales while offline, contact Nova support so the correct offline workflow can be confirmed for that setup. Once the internet is working and websites load, Nova Wave should reconnect.

Q: What type of hardware is needed for Nova Wave?

A: Start with a Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer with at least 4 GB RAM; an SSD is strongly recommended. Common peripherals are a thermal receipt printer, cash drawer, barcode scanner, and optional label printer. Card terminals depend on the merchant processor and are configured under Back Office > Locations > Settings > Edit > Merchant Services. Printer setup happens in Windows: install the printer, set it as the Windows default printer/default device, then test in Nova Wave.

Q: I want to buy hardware from Amazon but I am afraid of ordering the wrong thing. What should I do?

A: Send Nova support the exact model links before ordering so compatibility can be checked. For receipt printers, Star Micronics TSP100 series and Epson TM-T20/TM-T88IV are common supported options. Cash drawers usually connect to the receipt printer's RJ11 port, and label printers are for product labels, not normal customer receipts.

Q: My register computer is slow / freezing. How do I fix it?

A: Work through these in order. Restart the computer — this clears memory and usually fixes slowness on its own. Close any background programs (browsers, video players). Check for Windows updates — out-of-date systems get slower over time. Make sure you have at least 10 GB of free disk space. Run a quick antivirus scan to rule out malware. Nova runs best on Windows 10 or 11 with at least 4 GB of RAM and an SSD. If your computer is over 5 years old, it may be time for an upgrade.

Q: My computer is over 5 years old — should I replace it?

A: Probably yes. Nova runs best on a workstation with Windows 10 or 11, at least 4 GB of RAM, and an SSD. If yours is older than 5 years and feeling slow even after a restart and cleanup, give Nova support a call — they can recommend specs for a replacement.

Q: I have an unknown or off-brand device. How do I know which driver to install?

A: First confirm the device's brand and model from the device label, box, receipt, or Windows device listing. Then confirm the device is compatible with Windows. If it is Windows-compatible, download the driver from the manufacturer's official website for that exact model. Do not install drivers from random third-party driver sites. If you cannot confirm the model, Windows compatibility, or the correct manufacturer driver, call Nova support.

Q: Nova stopped working on my Mac (running through Parallels). What now?

A: Close Nova completely, then reopen it. If that doesn't fix it, restart Windows inside Parallels. Then verify the printer or device you're trying to use appears inside Windows — peripherals connected to macOS still need to be passed through correctly to Windows in Parallels.

Q: My printer doesn't show up in Parallels on my Mac. What do I check?

A: Peripherals connected to macOS need to be passed through to the Windows virtual machine in Parallels. Open Parallels' device settings and confirm the printer is set to connect to the Windows VM (not held by macOS). Then check inside Windows — open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners to confirm the printer appears there. If it doesn't pass through cleanly, call Nova support.

Q: How do I know if my internet is actually down vs. just Nova having trouble?

A: Open a web browser and try to load any website (Google, your store's website, etc.). If websites load, the internet is fine and Nova just needs a restart. If websites also fail to load, the internet itself is down — call your internet provider. Nova will reconnect once the internet is back.

Q: What are the minimum specs to run Nova well?

A: Nova runs best on a workstation with Windows 10 or 11, at least 4 GB of RAM, and an SSD (solid-state drive). Older hardware or mechanical drives will work but feel slower over time. For a full spec recommendation tailored to your store, call Nova support.
