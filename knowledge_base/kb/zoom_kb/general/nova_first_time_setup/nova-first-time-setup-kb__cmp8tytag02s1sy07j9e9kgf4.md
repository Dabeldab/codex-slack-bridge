Nova First Time Setup Overview

This article guides a new customer through the complete first-time setup of a Nova account — from sign-up through the four pillars of account configuration. Use this when a customer asks how to get started, has just signed up, is opening their account for the first time, or is configuring their first location.

What this guide covers
Phase	What happens
Sign-up	Create the Nova account from the public website or the direct order form
Post-signup	Automatic redirect to Back Office, trial begins, welcome modal appears
Storefront install	Optional at signup, can be installed any time later
Guided tour	Built-in walkthrough of Back Office navigation
Account setup	Four main configuration pillars: Locations, Products, Inventory, Users
The four setup pillars

After the new account is created, four configuration areas must be completed before the account is fully operational:

Location Settings — store name, address, tax rate, receipt customization, merchant services (covered in detail in this article)
Products — product catalog, categories, pricing, variants
Inventory — initial stock levels, inventory rules
Users — employees, roles, passwords, permissions

Customers can complete these in any order, but Location Settings is the recommended starting point because tax rates, receipt formatting, and payment processing depend on it.

Trial period

New accounts begin a trial period after registration. The remaining trial days are shown in the top-right corner of the Back Office (for example: "Your Trial End in 10 Days"). The trial countdown appears throughout the Back Office until the account is converted to a paid subscription.

The Company Username and Company Password (read this carefully)

Your welcome email contains two values that look like credentials. They are not your daily login credentials. They are installation credentials — used one time per device when you first set up the Storefront on a new computer or first add your account to the mobile app.

Field in the email	What it is	When you use it
Company Username	The unique identifier for your business account	When you add the account to a new Storefront install or to the mobile app
Company Password (Access Key)	The authentication key for adding your account to a device	At the same step as Company Username
Critical handling rules
Treat both values as sensitive. Anyone with the Company Username and Company Password can install Nova as your business on a new device. Do not share these publicly, post screenshots of them, or paste them into chat support tickets.
The Company Password may include = symbols at the start, end, or both. These are part of the key. They must be copied exactly. Do not strip them.
Watch for spaces. When copying from the email, make sure no leading or trailing spaces come along with the value.
These credentials are install-time only. Once your Storefront or mobile app has the account added, you will not enter them again on that device. Daily sign-in uses your personal credentials (covered in the next sections).

Trial Period and Billing

Every new Nova account starts on a 10-day trial with no payment required up front. This section answers every common question new customers ask about the trial and billing.

Trial basics
Detail	What it means
Length	10 days, starting from the day you sign up
Eligibility	New account signups only
Cost	Free during the 10 days
Credit card required	No — you can complete sign-up and use the trial without entering any payment details
Trial expiry date	Shown in your welcome email in the pink-outlined "Trial Expiry Date" box, and visible in the top-right corner of the Back Office as a countdown
What happens at the end of the trial

You have two paths at the end of the 10 days:

Path 1: You did not add a payment method
The account automatically closes at the end of the 10 days.
No amount is due. You owe nothing. There are no past-due bills, no penalties, and no collection.
All of your data is preserved internally. The account is closed, not deleted.
You are under no obligation to continue.
Path 2: You want to keep using Nova
Add a payment method by clicking Add Payment Method in the welcome email, or by going to the billing section in Back Office.
Payment is taken at the time of activation, which happens after the 10-day trial ends.
Nova is month-to-month prepaid — you pay for the month in advance, so there are never past-due amounts.
After payment, the account continues active and your trial countdown disappears.
Reactivating a closed account

If your trial ended, the account closed, and later you decide you want to use Nova:

You can reactivate at any time.
The reactivation step is simply to pay for the month — there is no separate reactivation fee.
All your previously-entered data (products, settings, etc.) is restored when you reactivate.
Important: trials and additional locations

The 10-day trial applies only to new account signups. If you already have a paid Nova account and you add a new or additional Location to that account, the new Location begins billing immediately — it does not receive its own 10-day trial.

Quick billing FAQs
Question	Short answer
Do I have to enter a credit card to start the trial?	No
Will I be charged automatically at the end of the trial?	No — if you do not add a payment method, the account auto-closes with no charge
If I don't continue, do I owe Nova anything?	No
When is the first payment due?	At activation, after the 10-day trial ends — only if you choose to continue
Is Nova month-to-month?	Yes, month-to-month prepaid
Are there past-due amounts ever?	No — because every month is prepaid, you can never have a past-due balance
Can I reactivate later if I let the account close?	Yes — just pay for the month and the account is reactivated with all data intact
Do additional Locations also get a 10-day trial?	No — only new account signups get the 10-day trial. Additional Locations start billing immediately

Understanding Location Name and Password
Name

The Name field is the location's display name. It appears:

On printed and emailed receipts
In all Back Office reports
In the top-bar Location selector
In the location list

Common naming patterns: city name, street address, or store number (for example: "St-Hyacinthe", "Main Street", "Store 02").

Password (optional)

The Password field is a secondary security layer on top of normal user authentication. It is separate from individual user passwords.

When a Location Password is set, even an authenticated user must provide the location password to access the location's Storefront. This is most useful in multi-location accounts where employees of one location should be physically prevented from logging into a different location's Storefront — even if they accidentally connect to a different terminal.

If the Location Password field is left blank, no additional location-level password is required (normal user authentication still applies).

Understanding Regions

A Region is an organizational grouping that allows business locations to be separated into different zones for reporting and accounting purposes.

Use cases for Regions
Use case	Example
Geographic separation	"East Coast" and "West Coast" Regions, each containing multiple locations
Brand separation	A franchise account with multiple brand banners, one Region per brand
Tax jurisdiction separation	Different state or province tax filing groupings
Reporting separation	Roll up sales reports by Region for executive review
Region behavior
Every location must belong to exactly one Region.
New accounts have a default "Region 1" already created — customers can rename it or add additional Regions as needed.
Regions are configured at Back Office → Locations → Regions.
Top-bar Region selector lets users switch between Regions to view location-specific data.
Regions in Customer Management

Important: Customer records are account-wide/global and are not scoped to a Region or Location in Customer Management. The Region context affects how invoices, layaways, and user reports are filtered — not the customer list itself.

Receipt Header Text

Appears at the bottom of every receipt. Typical content:

Tax Rate Configuration

Sales tax is calculated per location based on the Tax Rate value entered on General Info.

Setting the primary Tax Rate
Locate the Tax Rate section on General Info.
Enter the tax rate as a percentage in the Tax Rate field (e.g., enter 8.25 for 8.25%).
The % symbol is displayed inside the field and the value should be a decimal number with no % symbol.
Tax Display As

Some jurisdictions print tax under a non-standard name. Use the Tax Display As field to override the default "Tax" label:

Region	Common label
United States	Tax (default)
United Kingdom	VAT
Australia, New Zealand	GST
Canada	GST, HST, PST (depending on province)
European Union	VAT

If Tax Display As is left blank, receipts print "Tax" by default.

Second Tax Rate

Some jurisdictions require two taxes (for example, GST + PST in some Canadian provinces, or city + state tax in some US locations).

Use the 2nd Tax Rate section to configure the second tax:

Tax Rate 2 — percentage for the second tax
Tax2 Display As — custom label (e.g., PST, City Tax)

If 2nd Tax Rate is left at 0.00 or blank, only the primary tax applies.

External Card Machine Setup

Use this path when the location uses a standalone card terminal that is not connected to or integrated with Nova POS.

When to use
The customer has a card terminal provided by their bank or a third-party processor
Card transactions are processed entirely on the standalone terminal
Nova only needs to record the transaction (not process it)
Setup steps
On the Merchant Services tab, leave "No I am not" selected for the integrated payment question.
Scroll down to the Credit Card Settings section at the bottom of the tab.
Find the toggle labeled "Block use of external credit card machine."
Disable this toggle (toggle it to OFF). When this toggle is ON (red), the external card machine option is blocked at the POS. When OFF, the External payment method becomes available at the Storefront.
Click SAVE at the bottom-right of the page.
Why this matters

If the toggle is left ON (the default), cashiers cannot record external card transactions at checkout. The Storefront Payment screen's External button will be disabled or hidden. Customers using standalone card terminals will be unable to complete sales until this setting is changed.

Credit Card Settings and Security

The Credit Card Settings section at the bottom of the Merchant Services tab contains three toggles that control credit card behavior at the POS.

Toggle	What it does	Recommended state
Block use of external credit card machine	When ON, the External payment method is unavailable at the Storefront	OFF if using a standalone external card terminal; ON if exclusively using integrated processing or cash/check only
Allow the user to manually enter credit card information	When ON, cashiers can type card numbers directly into Nova when the integrated terminal fails	OFF for most accounts (this opens an avenue for fraud and should only be enabled with management approval)
Force the user to enter the card's CV information	When ON, manual entry requires the CVV from the back of the card	ON if manual entry is enabled (additional fraud prevention)
Security note

Manual credit card entry is historically the most common vector for employee fraud. Nova recommends keeping "Allow the user to manually enter credit card information" OFF unless the customer specifically needs it. If a customer requests manual entry to be enabled, document the request and confirm the business owner is aware before changing the setting.

Products Setup Overview

This is the second of the four setup pillars. Products are the items the business sells. Before products will appear on the Storefront, two things must be true:

The product record must be created (this article).
The product must be assigned to a location's inventory with a quantity (covered in the Inventory Setup section below).

A product created but not assigned to a location's inventory will not appear on the Storefront — this is the most common first-time setup mistake.

Product Default Price section
Field	Required	Validation
Product Cost Price	No	2 decimals, ≥ 0. The wholesale or supplier cost — used for margin calculations
Product Suggested Price	Yes	2 decimals, ≥ 0. The normal selling price. Empty field error: default pricing suggested must not be empty
Product Minimum Price	No	2 decimals, ≥ 0, must be ≤ Product Suggested Price. The discount floor / employee discount leeway

Understanding Fixed-Price vs Negotiable

This is one of the most important pricing decisions for each product.

Fixed-Price (Is Negotiable = OFF)

The product always sells at the Product Suggested Price. Cashiers cannot discount it at checkout. Use this for:

Standard retail items with a single price
Items with strict pricing policy
SKUs where employee discounting must be prevented

Recommended setup for fixed-price items:

Set Product Suggested Price to the normal selling price
Set Is Negotiable = OFF
Leave Product Minimum Price unused
Negotiable (Is Negotiable = ON)

The cashier can discount the product at checkout, down to the Product Minimum Price (or to $0.00 if Minimum Price is left blank). Use this for:

Items where price haggling is normal (specialty retail, art, gifts)
Items with promotional flexibility
Items where the cashier should have discount room

Important warning: If Is Negotiable = ON and Product Minimum Price = 0 or blank, a cashier may be able to sell the item for $0.00. Always set a Product Minimum Price for negotiable items, or use Is Negotiable = OFF for strict pricing.

Product Tax Settings

By default, products use the location's primary Tax Rate (set in Location Settings → General Info). Some products need special tax handling.

Tax controls on Single Product Details
Toggle / field	Purpose
Tax amount deducted from product price	When ON, the Suggested Price is treated as tax-inclusive and the tax is backed out from the total. When OFF (default), tax is added on top
Use product specific tax rate	When ON, this product overrides the location tax rate with its own rate from the Diff Tax Rate field
Diff Tax Rate (%)	The product-specific tax rate as a percentage. Active only when "Use product specific tax rate" is ON
When to use product-specific tax
A product is tax-exempt (set Use product specific tax rate ON, Diff Tax Rate = 0)
A product has a different tax category than the rest of the store (e.g., food has reduced tax in some jurisdictions)
A product requires a special tax (e.g., excise tax on tobacco or alcohol)

If the location's primary tax rate is correct for the product, leave both tax toggles OFF.

Product Commission Setup

Commission Policy controls how commission is paid to the cashier when this specific product is sold. This is a sensitive payroll-affecting setting and should be reviewed carefully.

The three options
Policy	Behavior
None	The product pays zero commission. Overrides any user-level commission for this product
Regular (default)	The product follows the user's normal commission settings. No special product-level commission rule
Extra Commission	Adds extra commission on top of the user's normal commission. The extra amount is calculated from net sold price (pre-tax, post-discount)
How to set product commission
On the Create Product or Edit Product page, find Commission Policy in Product Information.
Select None, Regular, or Extra Commission from the dropdown.
If Extra Commission is selected, enter the percentage in the Extra Commission(%) field.
Save the product.
Extra Commission validation
Whole numbers (integers) only
Allowed range: -100 to 100
Negative values reduce commission (act as a penalty on that product)
Error if invalid or blank: Error happened saving product: { "message": "Commission amount is a value between -100% and 100% different than 0" }
Important: Product Commission requires User Commission setup

For a user to receive product commission, two things must both be true:

The product has a Commission Policy of Regular or Extra Commission.
The user's record has the Product Commission toggle enabled in Users → Settings → USER SALARY.

If the user's Product Commission toggle is OFF, the user does NOT participate in product commission even when the product has a commission policy set. Make sure to enable Product Commission on the user when setting up users (covered in Users Setup section).

Commission caution
Incorrect commission settings can create unexpected overpayment, underpayment, employee disputes, or business margin loss.
Example: if a user earns 30% commission normally and the product is set to Extra Commission = 20%, that user receives 50% total commission on that sale.
Refunds reverse product commission for the user.
Review the payout impact before saving.
Inventory Options on a Product (Making it Sellable)

This is the section most commonly missed during first-time setup. Creating the product is not enough to make it appear on the Storefront — it must be assigned to a location's inventory.

Where Inventory Options is

The Inventory Options section is at the bottom of the Single Product Details tab (for single products) or at the bottom of each variant block (for variable products). Look for the heading "Inventory Options" with an "Is Trackable" toggle below it.

Is Trackable toggle
State	Behavior	When to use
ON (default)	Nova enforces quantity tracking. Selling decrements stock. Stock cannot go below 0 unless allowed
OFF	Product can still be sold, but Nova does not track quantity	Recommended for services (haircut, repair, consultation) that have no physical stock
Assign the product to a location
With Is Trackable ON (or OFF for services), find the Region dropdown.
Click the Region dropdown — a list of regions appears with Select all at the top.
Check the Region(s) where this product should be sold.
Click outside or close the dropdown — the selected Region count appears (e.g., "1 region selected").
Click the Select Location dropdown.
Check the location(s) within the selected Region(s).
Click ADD LOCATIONS (the button to the right of Select Location).

The location(s) appear in a table below with Qty, Max, Min columns.

Set Quantity, Max, and Min

For each location in the table:

Field	Purpose	Required
Qty	Current quantity in stock at this location	Required to make the product appear on Storefront (must be > 0 unless Is Trackable is OFF)
Max	The highest quantity the store wants to carry. Used as a reorder cap when generating purchase orders	Optional
Min	The lowest quantity before a low-stock alert fires (when alerts are enabled). Used to schedule reorders	Optional

Bulk inventory update: Above the location table is a Bulk inventory update section. Enter Qty, Max, Min once and click UPDATE to apply the same values to all locations in the table.

Important sellability rule: The product will not appear on the Storefront for a location until that location has been added with at least Qty entered (or Is Trackable is OFF). This catches almost every "why isn't my product showing up?" question.

What Max and Min do
Max is a cap used during purchase order workflows — when reordering, the system can target this number as the ceiling. Not required for daily sales.
Min triggers low-stock alerts (when the location has alerts enabled). When inventory drops below Min, the system notifies management to reorder.
Max and Min are per-location and per-product — they can be different at every store.
Neither field is required to make the product sellable. Set them only if the business uses purchase orders or low-stock alerts.

A t-shirt that comes in Blue, Green, Red, Orange
A water bottle that comes in Small, Medium, Large

Fixing Products that Don't Appear on the Storefront

The most common first-time setup question: "I created a product but it does not appear on my Storefront."

The cause is almost always one of these three:

The product was not assigned to a location (no inventory entry exists for that location).
The product was assigned to a location but the quantity (Qty) is 0 or blank.
The product's Active Product toggle is OFF.
Fix from the product record
Go to Back Office → Products → Settings.
Search for the product and click Edit on its row.
Confirm Active Product toggle is ON (top-right of Product Information).
Scroll to Inventory Options at the bottom of Single Product Details (or each variant for variable products).
Make sure Is Trackable is ON (or OFF for services).
Confirm the correct Region and Location are in the location table. If not, select Region → Select Location → click ADD LOCATIONS.
Enter a Qty greater than 0 in the location row.
Click UPDATE on the location row.
Click SUBMIT at the bottom-right to save the product.
Refresh the Storefront to verify the product now appears.
Fix from Inventory Management

When the product is already in the system but missing from a specific location, or when many products need bulk quantity updates, use Inventory Management:

Go to Back Office → Inventory → Management.
Use the search/filter to find the product or location.
Update Qty (and optionally Max/Min) directly in the table.
Save changes.
Updating Inventory Quantities

Stock levels change as products are sold, received, or counted. Update inventory whenever the physical stock doesn't match what Nova shows.

From a single product
Back Office → Products → Settings → Edit on the product.
Scroll to Inventory Options.
In the location table, update Qty for the affected location.
Click UPDATE on the row.
SUBMIT to save the product.
From Inventory Management (bulk)

Use this when many products need quantity adjustments, or when reconciling after a physical count.

Back Office → Inventory → Management.
Filter by location, category, or vendor to narrow the list.
Update Qty directly in the table cells.
Save the changes.
When to do a Daily Count

For accurate inventory, perform a periodic Daily Count to reconcile physical stock with Nova:

Back Office → Inventory → Daily Count.
Select the location to count.

Submit the count — Nova adjusts the inventory and produces a reconciliation report showing discrepancies.

USER SALARY (optional but important for commission-paid employees)

If the employee earns commission or hourly wages calculated by Nova, configure USER SALARY before saving.

After completing USER INFORMATION, click USER SALARY tab.
Enable the relevant pay components:
Component	When to use
Flat Commission	Most common — a percentage of daily net sales (e.g., 10%). Calculated daily on pre-tax, post-discount sales
Hourly	Hourly rate paid by the minute. Uses exact worked time
Include break in total hour count	Default OFF. When OFF, break time is not paid
Base Salary	Flat amount paid weekly or monthly. Used with Added to Salary and Added on fields
Product Commission	Default OFF. Toggle ON to include this user in product commission plans. Without this ON, the user does NOT participate in product-level commission even if a product has Commission Policy set
Higher of Commission or Hourly	Compares the day's commission total to the day's hourly total and pays the higher one
Commission Steps	Tiered commission rates based on daily sales thresholds
Important: Enable Product Commission for cashiers

For cashiers to receive product commission on items configured with Commission Policy = Regular or Extra Commission, the user's Product Commission toggle in USER SALARY must be ON. This is the most common cause of "the cashier did not get their commission" complaints during first-time setup.

Recommended Salary Setup for Common Cases

Most businesses use one of these patterns. Set them on the USER SALARY tab.

Pattern 1: Commission-only cashier
Flat Commission: enter percentage (e.g., 10 for 10%)
Product Commission: ON if any products have Commission Policy set
Everything else: leave OFF
Pattern 2: Hourly employee
Hourly: enter hourly rate (e.g., 18.00)
Include break in total hour count: choose based on the business's policy
Everything else: leave OFF
Pattern 3: Hybrid (hourly + commission, paid the higher)
Hourly: enter hourly rate
Flat Commission: enter commission percentage
Higher of Commission or Hourly: ON
This pays whichever is higher on a given day
Pattern 4: Salaried manager
Base Salary: enter the flat amount
Added to Salary: choose Weekly or Monthly
Added on: choose the day of week (weekly) or day of month (monthly) for payment timing
Optionally: Expense to Location to attribute the salary as a location expense

Bot Logic: Products, Inventory, and Users
IF user asks how to add a product → route to Back Office → Products → Settings → Add New Product. Minimum required: Product Name, Category, Product Suggested Price.
IF user asks why a product is not appearing on the Storefront → check (1) Active Product toggle, (2) Inventory Options has Region/Location assigned, (3) Qty is greater than 0.
IF user asks about fixed-price products → state Is Negotiable = OFF makes the product fixed-price; do not rely on Suggested = Minimum because Is Negotiable = OFF is the documented control.
IF user asks about products selling for $0.00 → state Is Negotiable = ON with Minimum Price = 0 or blank allows $0 sales. Set Minimum Price or turn Is Negotiable OFF.
IF user asks about product commission → explain Commission Policy options (None, Regular, Extra Commission), include the caution about payroll impact, and remind them that the user's Product Commission toggle in USER SALARY must also be ON.
IF user asks how to create a product with sizes/colors → route to Variable Product Details tab → Add New Option → choose Family → add option values → CREATE OPTIONS → configure each variant.
IF user asks how to set quantities → route to Inventory Options inside each product (or each variant) → Region → Select Location → ADD LOCATIONS → enter Qty → UPDATE.
IF user asks what Max and Min do → Max is the purchase-order ceiling, Min triggers low-stock alerts. Neither is required for sellability.
IF user asks how to add an employee → route to Back Office → Users → Settings → Add New User. Minimum required: User Name.
IF user asks why an employee didn't get commission on a product → check (1) Commission Policy on the product, (2) Product Commission toggle in user's USER SALARY.
IF user asks how to delete a user/product → state Nova does not delete; users are deactivated, products use Active Product OFF.
Answer Safety: Products, Inventory, Users
Do
Tell users that creating a product is not the same as making it sellable — inventory assignment with Qty is also required.
Use exact UI labels: Add New Product, Product Suggested Price, Is Negotiable, Commission Policy, Extra Commission(%), Inventory Options, Is Trackable, ADD LOCATIONS, CREATE OPTIONS, COPY FOR ALL, USER SALARY, Product Commission.
Warn that Product Commission is a sensitive payroll-affecting setting and review impact before saving.
Tell users that "Copy for all" overwrites existing variant values.
Remind that the Product Commission toggle in USER SALARY must be ON for users to receive product commission.
Tell users that Nova deactivates rather than deletes products and users.
Suggest Submit & Clone or Submit & New for bulk product creation.
Do Not
Do not tell users to set Suggested = Minimum to make a product fixed-price. The documented control is Is Negotiable = OFF.
Do not invent Commission Policy options beyond None, Regular, Extra Commission.
Do not promise commission calculations beyond daily basis — Nova does not auto-calculate weekly or monthly commission.
Do not tell users that products with Is Trackable = OFF cannot be sold — they can still be sold, just without quantity tracking.
Do not skip the inventory step when answering "how do I add a product" — always include the sellability rule.
Do not provide variant limits beyond what is documented: 2 families, 10 values per family, 100 variants total.
Do not advise enabling Product Commission on a user without confirming the business intends them to participate in product-level commission plans.
